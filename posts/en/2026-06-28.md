## OpenAI Begins Limited Preview of Next-Gen AI "GPT-5.6"

OpenAI has started a limited preview of its next-generation high-performance AI model "GPT-5.6" series. Consisting of three models, including the flagship model "Sol," it will be provided to a trusted few partners at the request of the U.S. federal government. OpenAI argues that such a government pre-screening process "should not become a permanent standard," raising a fundamental question about the nature of government involvement in AI development.

## Anthropic's Partial Re-deployment of AI Model "Mythos" Approved by U.S. Government

U.S. AI company Anthropic announced that the U.S. government has approved the partial re-deployment of its AI model "Claude Mythos 5," which had been suspended due to export restrictions, to some U.S. organizations. However, "Claude Fable 5" remains suspended, and discussions are ongoing for its general availability. This highlights the current situation where the U.S. government imposes strict regulations on the use of certain high-performance AI models from a national security perspective.

## Literary Giant Margaret Atwood Points Out AI's Challenge is "Garbage In, Garbage Out"

Renowned author Margaret Atwood, known for works like *The Handmaid's Tale*, described a fundamental problem with AI as "garbage in, garbage out." This refers to the core challenge in AI development: the quality of AI output heavily depends on the quality of the input data. If AI is trained with biased or low-quality data, it may also produce biased or inaccurate information.
[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/958715/margaret-atwood-ai-problem-garbage-in-garbage-out)
Our lives affected: Awareness of ethical AI use and the importance of data quality will further increase, prompting deeper consideration of AI's impact on society.
