## Anthropic's Key AI Models Suspended by Government Order

Anthropic, a major AI development company, had its key AI models, "Fable 5" and "Mythos 5," suspended globally following a national security order from the U.S. government. Reports indicate that this decision is also backed by concerns previously expressed by Amazon's cybersecurity research and CEO Andy Jassy.

This development indicates that the impact of high-performance AI models on national security is becoming a reality, highlighting the increasing importance of balancing safety and regulation in future AI development and usage.

Impact on Our Lives: There's a possibility of stronger AI regulations at the national level, which will affect the scope of AI technology use and international cooperation.

## KPMG Withdraws AI Usage Report Due to "Hallucinations"

A report on AI usage published by consulting giant KPMG was withdrawn after it was found to contain AI "hallucinations" (the phenomenon where AI generates factually incorrect information).

This incident once again highlights a deep-seated challenge concerning the reliability of information generated by AI. It suggests that strict verification and human judgment are indispensable when using AI, especially in situations involving critical information or decision-making.

Impact on Our Lives: We will be required to develop a habit of not blindly accepting information from AI and always verifying its reliability.

[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/kpmg-pulls-report-on-ai-usage-due-to-apparent-hallucinations/)

## OpenAI Under Investigation by Multiple State Attorneys General

It has been revealed that OpenAI, a leading AI company that develops ChatGPT and other technologies, is under investigation by several state attorneys general regarding its advertising policies, handling of health data, and other issues.

As the social impact of AI technology expands, aspects of governance such as corporate ethics, data privacy, and transparency are being rigorously scrutinized. Such investigations into major AI companies could accelerate the trend toward stronger regulation across the entire AI industry.

Impact on Our Lives: Discussions around data privacy and personal information protection when using AI services will become more active, and a safer AI usage environment is expected to be established.

## Individual Develops "Garden" Management App Using Google Gemini

According to an article in The Verge, a case was featured where a user utilized Google's LLM (Large Language Model) "Gemini" to quickly develop an app for managing their home garden. The user reportedly created a functional app by simply providing Gemini with a lengthy prompt and also leveraged its bug-fixing capabilities.

This example demonstrates the potential for AI tools like LLMs to allow individuals to easily bring ideas to life and solve everyday problems, even without specialized programming knowledge. It suggests a future where AI significantly enhances our creative activities and productivity.

Impact on Our Lives: The barrier to programming will be lowered, and anyone may be able to create their own useful tools and apps using AI.

## Apple Fully Integrates AI Image Editing Features into iOS

Apple has announced the introduction of long-awaited AI photo editing tools into iOS. This will enable iPhone users to directly utilize advanced AI features such as "reframe," "extend," and "clean up" for photos on their devices.

With AI editing capabilities natively integrated into the iPhone, one of the world's most widely used cameras, general users will be able to edit professional-level photos more easily. This marks a significant step towards AI being seamlessly integrated into our daily tools.

Impact on Our Lives: Photos taken with smartphones will be able to be edited more beautifully and attractively with the power of AI, making SNS posts and sharing memories even more enjoyable.

## Hollywood Potentially Utilizing Generative AI Beyond "Vanilla" Models

Regarding the use of generative AI (AI that generates text, images, etc.) in the film industry, cases were introduced where Hollywood filmmakers are going beyond "vanilla models" (generic, uncustomized models) that merely involve entering prompts, and instead are training custom-built models like Google's Veo and Imagen for film production.

This suggests that generative AI is being integrated more deeply and professionally into the filmmaking process, serving not just as an auxiliary tool but as a core element of creative expression. While AI's fusion with art holds the potential for new visual expressions, it will also deepen discussions about its impact and ethics.

Impact on Our Lives: As AI-generated visual content diversifies and the range of expression in films and dramas expands, the entertainment we experience may become richer.
