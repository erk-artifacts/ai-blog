## Midjourney V8 Announced with 5x Speed Boost and 2K Resolution Major Evolution

The image generation AI service **Midjourney** released the initial version of its new model "V8" on March 18. The biggest feature of this version upgrade is that processing speed has been increased 5x compared to the previous version. Additionally, it now supports output at 2K resolution, making it possible to generate higher quality images.

Midjourney has long been known as an industry-leading image generation AI, but with the arrival of V8, further acceleration in adoption across creative industries and marketing fields is expected.

**Impact on our lives:** Image creation for SNS and design work will become even more efficient, bringing creative work closer to everyday use.

## OpenAI Announces Compact Models "GPT-5.4 mini/nano" for Sub-agents

**OpenAI** announced on March 17 the compact AI models "GPT-5.4 mini" and "GPT-5.4 nano." These models are designed for sub-agents (auxiliary AI agents) and deliver efficient, lightweight processing.

This announcement comes at a time when demand is growing for AI agents that perform not just large-scale language processing tasks, but also specific auxiliary roles. This enables more companies to leverage AI technology.

**Impact on our lives:** As lighter, faster AI becomes more prevalent, AI applications on devices like smartphones will become more practical.

## "Data Center" Powered by Human Brain Cells - Australian Startup Begins Construction

It has been revealed that Australian startup **Cortical Labs** is building the world's first biological data center powered by human brain cells in Melbourne and Singapore. The most surprising aspect is that the system's power consumption is kept below that of a calculator.

This represents a breakthrough beyond the limitations of conventional silicon-based computing and marks the emergence of an entirely new paradigm of biological computation. **Biocomputing** is attracting attention as a potential solution to energy problems in the AI era.

**Impact on our lives:** Data center power consumption reduction could lead to lower energy costs and subsequent price reductions in IT and AI services.

## Pentagon Plans AI Training on Classified Data - Development of Military-Specialized Models

The **U.S. Department of Defense (Pentagon)** is proceeding with plans to establish a secure environment for training on classified data, allowing generative AI companies (such as Anthropic's Claude) to develop military-specialized models.

AI models are already being used to answer questions in classified settings, but the plan going forward is to train those models on classified data to build AI optimized for military use. This is an important case demonstrating that AI practical applications have reached the level of national security.

**Impact on our lives:** As AI's military applications advance, international discussions on AI regulation and security standards will accelerate.

## AI-Driven Development Increases Human Responsibility - The Final Safeguard for Quality Assurance

The proliferation of coding agents (**AI-driven development**) has significantly improved code generation productivity. However, this simultaneously raises questions of responsibility for AI-generated code.

There is a growing trend where responsibility for ensuring the quality of AI-generated code ultimately falls on humans. In other words, while AI increases efficiency in code creation, the responsibility for deploying it weighs more heavily than ever on developers. This needs to be recognized as a new challenge in the AI era.

**Impact on our lives:** Demand for higher reliability in AI-developed systems and applications will grow, increasing the importance of quality assurance roles.

## Building Data Infrastructure in Manufacturing - "Classmethod SmartFactory AI Base" Launches

Classmethod Inc. began providing **"Classmethod SmartFactory AI Base,"** a data platform supporting acceleration of DX (digital transformation) in manufacturing, on March 13.

This platform aggregates diverse data from factory floors to the cloud and performs visualization, analysis, and notification in a secure environment. As a practical solution combining **manufacturing and AI**, it is expected to contribute to DX promotion in Japan's manufacturing facilities while improving production efficiency and reducing costs.

**Impact on our lives:** Improvements in manufacturing production efficiency will enable higher quality products to be provided at lower costs.
