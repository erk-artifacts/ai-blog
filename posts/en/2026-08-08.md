## OpenAI Partially Halts Development of Next Model "Astra" – Citing Potential for Dangerous Cyber Capabilities
OpenAI has announced the possibility that the cyber capabilities of its next flagship model, "Astra," currently under development, may have reached "Critical," the highest level of its safety guidelines, and has halted some development activities. This is based on concerns that Astra might possess the ability to autonomously execute cyberattacks. OpenAI has stated its policy is to not suppress the capabilities but rather to collaborate with government agencies and external organizations to verify and provide safety measures.
[→ Read Original Article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2608/08/2000000459/)
[→ Read Official Announcement (OpenAI News)](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities)
*Impact on Our Lives: The safety of advanced AI models will be subject to stricter scrutiny, leading to new regulations and technological developments to protect our digital society.

## Anthropic Relaxes Biology Restrictions for "Claude Fable 5"
Anthropic has announced that it has relaxed excessive protective features in the field of biology for its AI model, "Claude Fable 5." This is expected to significantly reduce false positives for harmless questions and fallback to less capable models (Less Capable Model) that previously occurred due to an overemphasis on safety. While maintaining restrictions on specialized dual-use research (Dual-use research, research applicable to both military and non-military purposes), convenience for general health and educational uses will improve.
[→ Read Original Article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2608/08/2000000460/)
*Impact on Our Lives: AI may become more accurate and useful in providing information, potentially accelerating AI adoption in various aspects of life, such as healthcare and education.

## Cloudflare Announces "Kitesurf," a Browser for AI Agents
Cloudflare has announced "Kitesurf," a cloud-hosted browser specifically for AI agents (Agent AI, AI that autonomously performs tasks). This browser is designed to efficiently execute AI-driven web automation tasks with fewer computing resources than traditional Chromium-based browsers used by humans. This will enable developers to build browser-based AI agents more efficiently.
[→ Read Original Article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/cloudflare-launches-kitesurf-a-browser-built-for-ai-agents/)
*Impact on Our Lives: AI's ability to collect and process web information will improve, potentially making automated services and applications more commonplace.

## Rippling Develops "AI Spend Console," Its Own AI Usage Cost Tracking Tool
After recommending AI usage to its employees resulted in significant costs within months, Rippling developed an internal tool called "AI Spend Console" based on that experience. This tool aims to track AI spending by individual employees and teams, and to visualize ROI (Return On Investment).
[→ Read Original Article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/after-rippling-blew-millions-on-ai-in-months-it-built-an-employee-roi-tool/)
*Impact on Our Lives: As AI adoption expands within businesses, cost management and ROI optimization will become crucial, making the business application of AI more strategic.
