## Anthropic to Apply "Invisible Watermarks" to Claude-Generated Text

AI development company Anthropic has announced its policy to embed "invisible digital watermarks" in text generated by its AI chatbot "Claude." This is in response to the transparency obligations of the "EU AI Act" (AI Regulation Act in the EU), which came into effect on August 2nd, and will be applied to models and services worldwide, including Japan. These watermarks will enable the identification of AI-generated text and the detection of alterations.

Impact on Our Lives: It will become easier to distinguish between AI-generated and human-created information, aiding in fake news countermeasures and copyright protection, thereby improving AI reliability.

## AI Tool "SOIN-L" Launched to Prevent Caregiving-Related Job Quitting

CDI Corporation has released "SOIN-L," an AI solution that supports the balance between work and caregiving. This tool provides individual support features for employees and risk management features for HR departments within a single system. AI offers concrete solutions to societal challenges such as job attrition due to caregiving and reduced productivity.

Impact on Our Lives: By providing concrete support for the major contemporary challenge of balancing caregiving and work, AI contributes to reducing individual burdens and fostering sustainable corporate growth, thereby assisting in supporting diverse work styles.

## Limitations of Transformers and Next-Generation LLM Approaches

Currently, Transformer models, which form the basis of major LLMs (Large Language Models), are noted for issues such as increased computational load and power consumption when processing long texts. In response to this "Transformer problem," several startup companies are challenging themselves to develop next-generation LLMs using four new technological approaches. This is crucial research and development aimed at further enhancing AI performance.

Impact on Our Lives: As AI's technical bottlenecks are resolved and AI capable of efficiently handling longer and more complex information is developed, it may be able to support our complex thought processes and creative activities more deeply.

## Game Developer Saber Denies "AI Replacing Writers"

The CEO of Saber Interactive, developer of the new game 'Rideshare Stimulator,' has denied claims by a former lead writer that writers were replaced by AI such as ChatGPT. This news once again sheds light on the role of AI in the creative industry, as well as the accompanying ethical discussions and employment issues.

Impact on Our Lives: This will spark deeper discussions across the industry regarding collaboration and role-sharing between AI and human creators, impacting future work styles and the shape of the creative industry.
