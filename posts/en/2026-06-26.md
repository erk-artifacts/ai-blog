## AMD's Open Strategy Shakes Up the AI Infrastructure Market
In the AI infrastructure market, particularly in the GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) sector, NVIDIA has long held a dominant position. However, AMD is challenging this stronghold with an open approach under its "Helios" strategy. A growing number of hyperscalers (companies operating large-scale data centers) are adopting AMD's GPUs to avoid the risks of relying solely on NVIDIA.
Impact on Our Lives: Increased competition could diversify AI technologies and potentially improve the cost-effectiveness of the services and products we use.

## Principles for Safely Using Generative AI: "Copy-Pasting Anything Is Dangerous!"
While the use of generative AI (AI that automatically creates text and images) is expanding, caution is required in its application. Specifically, indiscriminately copy-pasting confidential company information or personal data into AI poses a risk of information leakage. The article explains the types of data that should not be input into generative AI and how to use the "secret conversation" feature for temporary use without leaving a history.
Impact on Our Lives: Knowledge for safely and effectively utilizing generative AI is essential for enhancing information security awareness and protecting our digital lives.
