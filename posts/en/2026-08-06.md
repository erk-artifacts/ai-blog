## Workman Streamlines Product Photography with Image-Generating AI!
Apparel giant Workman has introduced image-generating AI (artificial intelligence) into its product photography operations, significantly boosting operational efficiency. Notably, by having AI take over product photography that couldn't be completed in time, the open rate for app notifications has also reportedly increased 1.5 times. This is a good example of AI producing practical effects even in creative fields.
Impact on our daily lives: Products familiar to us will be delivered more attractively and quickly as information, powered by AI.

## Demis Hassabis of Google DeepMind Appointed Chairman, to Focus on AGI
Demis Hassabis, formerly CEO of Google's AI research division Google DeepMind, has been newly appointed Chairman and Chief Scientist of Alphabet. He will entrust Gemini development to his successor, and Hassabis will reportedly focus on higher-level AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) research. This is a significant personnel change that will influence the direction of AI development.
Impact on our daily lives: AGI research, which may shape our future, holds the potential to accelerate further.

## Google AI Maestro Jeff Dean Goes Independent, Establishes New Company to Automate AI Experiments
Jeff Dean, who has long led AI research as Google's Chief Scientist, has announced his departure from Google to establish a new company, 'Discovery Loop.' This public benefit corporation aims to automate the entire AI experimentation and validation process by leveraging frontier AI models (state-of-the-art AI models) and computational infrastructure. Google will also support it as an initial investor.
Impact on our daily lives: The AI research and development cycle could accelerate dramatically, potentially leading to a future where new AI technologies are commercialized more quickly.

## JR East to Conduct Generative AI PoC at 'Midori-no-Madoguchi,' Aiming for Operational Efficiency and Improved Customer Experience
JR East has commenced a proof-of-concept experiment utilizing generative AI (AI that automatically generates text and images) at its 'Midori-no-Madoguchi' ticket offices. By accurately capturing customer requests with voice recognition and organizing ticketing information, the aim is to streamline station staff operations, reduce customer waiting times, and improve service. The use of AI in public transportation is likely to bring about tangible changes for us.
Impact on our daily lives: Purchasing tickets and making inquiries at stations could become smoother and more stress-free.

## Fujitsu Partners with 3 Major Industrial Robot Makers to Develop Common Platform for Physical AI
Fujitsu has announced a partnership with three major Japanese industrial robot manufacturers—FANUC, Yaskawa Electric, and Kawasaki Heavy Industries—in the field of physical AI (AI that moves physical machines like robots). Leveraging NVIDIA's technology, they aim to jointly develop a common software platform for physical AI to accelerate the adoption of AI in industrial robots.
Impact on our daily lives: Robots in various fields such as manufacturing and logistics will become smarter and more autonomous, contributing to increased productivity and solving labor shortages.
[→ Read the original article (Nikkei xTECH)](https://xtech.nikkei.com/atcl/nxt/mag/nc/18/020800017/072901478/)

## Mizuho FG and NVIDIA to Verify AI in On-Premise Environment, Accelerating AI Utilization in Finance
Mizuho Financial Group has announced that it will jointly verify AI model and AI agent (AI that autonomously executes specific tasks) execution and management technologies in an on-premise environment (company-owned server environment) with NVIDIA. This is expected to enhance the safety and efficiency of AI utilization in the financial sector.
Impact on our daily lives: Our financial services could become more highly personalized and secure through AI.
