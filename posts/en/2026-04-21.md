## Amazon Expands Investment in Anthropic to Maximum of $33 Billion

Amazon announced it is expanding its total investment in AI company Anthropic to a maximum of **$33 billion**. In this strategic partnership, Anthropic has also signed a major contract to spend over $100 billion on AWS. Large-scale computing resources, including the latest AI chip "Trainium3," have been secured to address rapidly growing AI demand.

> **Key Point**: Major IT companies are accelerating large-scale AI investments, making it clear that securing computing resources has become a competitive axis in AI development.

**Impact on Our Lives**: AI tools like Claude through AWS are becoming more accessible, with increased potential for integration into various services.

## Code Review Burden Surges with AI-Generated Code, Creating New Challenges in Development

As the use of AI-generated code (program code automatically generated by LLMs) expands, serious issues have come to light. According to surveys, **approximately 9 out of 10 code reviewers report increased workload**, with the following issues reported:

- **78.6% have experienced bug fixes caused by AI**
- Situations where the code authors cannot explain the code they wrote
- Difficulty in maintaining code review quality

Development teams now face the challenge of balancing AI-generated code efficiency with quality management, and an increasing number of companies are considering new guidelines and tool implementations.

**Impact on Our Lives**: As software and application quality maintenance becomes critical, extended bug fix periods and enhanced security risk countermeasures become increasingly necessary.

## "Sales Copilot" AI Agent for SES Providers Launches Service

A new AI solution called **"Sales Copilot"** for SES (Systems Engineering Services) providers has begun service. This AI agent leverages a database of 4 million companies across Japan to achieve complete automation of sales activities:

- **Automatic prospecting research**
- **End-to-end sales support through appointment acquisition**
- Reduced workload for sales teams

An era where sales activities are automated by AI agents has arrived, and the roles and required skills of sales professionals are expected to change dramatically.

**Impact on Our Lives**: B2B sales processes will become more efficient, enabling faster and more accurate business matching.

## Sales Floor Insight AI "Front Agent" Appears at Expo

The conversational insight AI **"Front Agent"** will be exhibited at the Marketing Strategy Planning Expo being held at Tokyo Big Sight. This AI was developed to leverage the "raw voice" of the sales floor into marketing strategy:

- Automatically extract customer needs from conversations with sales representatives
- AI supports marketing strategy development
- Accelerates conventional qualitative research

An era is approaching where valuable information obtained on the sales floor is structured by AI and directly connected to management strategy.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Companies will be able to understand customer needs more accurately, leading to more personalized products and services tailored to individuals.
