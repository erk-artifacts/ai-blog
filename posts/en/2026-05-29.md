## AI Radio Program Generator "AI Radio Production Studio" Enters Full-Scale Operation Phase

The "AI Radio Production Studio," a service that automatically generates radio programs using AI (Artificial Intelligence), secured its first paying users just two weeks after its beta release and has now transitioned into its full-scale operation phase with approximately 30 users. This news is clear evidence that AI is beginning to provide practical value in creative fields, especially in media content production. Being able to produce high-quality radio content with minimal effort, the service is expected to attract demand from a wide range of users, from individual creators to small and medium-sized enterprises.

Even individual creators and small to medium-sized businesses will be able to easily create professional-quality content with the help of AI.

## Glean's Enterprise AI Search Thrives, Exceeding $300 Million in Annual Revenue

Glean, an enterprise AI search startup, has surpassed $300 million (approximately 47 billion yen) in annual revenue. While many tech giants are entering the same field, Glean has grown primarily by touting "AI-driven budget reduction" as its key selling point. It is being lauded for its contribution to cost reduction by streamlining information retrieval within companies and improving employee productivity, indicating that AI is starting to be recognized not just as a new technology, but as a tool directly linked to solving specific business challenges and improving ROI (Return on Investment).

Information discovery within companies will be dramatically streamlined, and wasted effort will be reduced, potentially improving our working environment.

## YouTube Premium Adds New Features to Enhance Podcast Experience with AI

YouTube has announced three new podcast-related features for Premium users. Among them, the "Auto speed" feature, which uses AI (Artificial Intelligence) to automatically adjust playback speed, is particularly noteworthy. This allows users to enjoy podcasts at an optimal speed, tailored to the content and their own concentration levels. Additionally, "On-the-go mode" for comfortable operation while out and about, and "Ask Music" (not yet available in Japan) for finding recommendations with conversational AI, have been introduced to enhance the podcast listener experience. This can be seen as an example of AI further advancing the personalization of media consumption.

As we enjoy podcasts and audiobooks, AI will provide a more comfortable and personalized listening experience.

## Advocating "ROC": Preventive Security Operations in the AI Era

Macnica has announced a new preventive security framework utilizing "Frontier AI" amidst the increasing complexity of corporate cybersecurity risks due to the evolution of AI (Artificial Intelligence). It advocates for a new operational model called "ROC (Risk Operation Center)," shifting from the traditional "chase all risks" approach to focusing solely on risks truly critical to the company. This enables more efficient security measures with limited resources, as AI enhances threat prioritization and analysis. This can be seen as an example of how AI will transform risk management in the security sector.

AI will become a "smart guardian" for our digital safety, and with the establishment of a more efficient and robust security environment, we will be able to use online services with greater peace of mind.
