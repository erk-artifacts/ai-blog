## Netflix to Utilize Generative AI in 300 Titles! Examples Show Production Costs Halved

Netflix announced in its Q2 earnings report that it plans to use generative AI (AI technology that automatically generates text, images, and audio, etc.) in approximately 300 titles to be distributed this year. Its use is primarily advancing in post-production (editing work after filming), with some cases showing production at twice the speed and half the cost compared to conventional methods. The production costs saved by this are expected to be reinvested in further content creation.

Impact on Our Lives: Generative AI shows the potential to significantly reduce time and cost in the creative industry, enabling the creation of more works.
