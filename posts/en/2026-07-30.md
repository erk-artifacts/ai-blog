## Thinking Machines Lab Co-founder to Return to OpenAI
Lilian Weng, co-founder of Thinking Machines Lab, a key player in the forefront of AI development, has announced her return to her former workplace, OpenAI. At OpenAI, she will lead a research team focused on "recursive self-improvement of AI," where AI learns and improves on its own. This move, coming right after OpenAI announced its open-weight model "Inkling," is expected to significantly impact future AI research.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2607/30/2000000286/)
Impact on our lives: The cutting edge of AI research will accelerate, potentially leading to the earlier emergence of smarter AI.

Technology giant Microsoft has clearly signaled its intention to engage in more direct competition than ever before with leading AI development companies OpenAI and Anthropic. Microsoft plans to actively deploy its self-developed AI models and services to strengthen its leadership in the AI market.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/29/microsoft-is-openly-competing-with-openai-anthropic-more-than-ever/)

## Microsoft Sees Increased Revenue and Profit Driven by Azure and AI Demand, Copilot Also Strong
Microsoft's latest financial results announced significant increases in revenue and profit, driven by the growth of its cloud service Azure and robust demand for AI-related businesses. The company plans to further expand its investment in AI infrastructure.

When Samsung announced its new foldable smartphones and other products in London, it showcased an innovative concept called "Agentic AI," which operates autonomously beyond the confines of the OS (operating system). This suggests a future where smartphones understand user intentions and actively perform tasks.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/423/4423126/?rss)

## Mark Zuckerberg Predicts Billions Will Have AI Agents Within Five Years
Mark Zuckerberg, CEO of Meta, has predicted that billions of people will have personal "AI agents" (AI that acts as a personal proxy) within the next five years. Meta is investing heavily in AI infrastructure, and this prediction forms the core of the company's AI strategy.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/29/mark-zuckerberg-predicts-that-billions-of-people-will-have-personal-ai-agents-in-five-years/)
Impact on our lives: Much of our daily lives could be supported by AI agents, making AI an indispensable presence, from searching for information to executing complex tasks.

## AI Search Industry Report: AI Citation Visibility and Google Fine
According to the latest monthly AI Search Industry Report, progress is being made in "citation visibility" (disclosure of sources) for AI-generated information. It was also revealed that the EU (European Union) has imposed a fine of approximately $1 billion on Google, indicating the growing importance of AI, ethics, and regulation.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/423/4423298/?rss)
Impact on our lives: The reliability of AI-generated information will improve, allowing users to access more accurate information, while AI development companies will be required to be more transparent.

## Microsoft Logs Revenue from Anthropic Investment, OpenAI Investment a 'Mixed Bag'
Microsoft's quarterly earnings report included interesting information regarding its investments in AI startups. While it recorded $3.2 billion in revenue from its investment in rival AI lab Anthropic, its investment in OpenAI was described as a "mixed bag." This illustrates the complexity of investment strategies by major corporations in the AI sector.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/29/microsoft-logs-3-2b-from-anthropic-investment-but-openai-was-a-mixed-bag/)

## Meta to Increase Capital Expenditure to Up To $145 Billion for Expanded AI Investment
Meta's latest financial results showed increased revenue, but net income decreased due to higher capital expenditure from its large-scale AI investment expansion. Under its "Meta Compute" strategy, which includes data center co-development, the company plans to invest a massive amount of up to $145 billion in AI infrastructure, demonstrating its strong commitment to the AI sector.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2607/30/2000000284/)
Impact on our lives: Meta's substantial investment in AI infrastructure could lead to the development of more advanced AI models and services, significantly evolving our social media and metaverse experiences.
