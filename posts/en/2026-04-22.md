## Meta Begins Recording Employee Keystrokes for AI Training

Meta has developed a new internal tool and announced plans to **convert employee mouse operations and button clicks into data for AI model training**. This approach enables large-scale collection of actual human operational patterns as learning data for AI.

While this initiative enhances corporate AI development efficiency, it also raises concerns about employee privacy. Meta faces demands for explanation regarding the transparency and ethical aspects of this undertaking.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Privacy standards in the workplace may be redefined, and this could become an impetus for forming industry standards regarding the ethical acquisition of AI training data.

## SpaceX Acquires Option to Buy AI Coding Company Cursor for $6 Billion

SpaceX, led by Elon Musk, has entered into a contract that **includes an option to acquire the automated programming platform "Cursor" for $6 billion (approximately 900 billion yen), as well as an option to pay a $1 billion fee**. Cursor is gaining attention as an **AI-powered coding assistance tool** and may be incorporated into xAI's tool development strategy.

This major acquisition option is seen as a strategic move to strengthen xAI's AI development capabilities ahead of SpaceX's IPO. The acquisition of Cursor is expected to dramatically enhance xAI's AI coding capabilities.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Competition in AI-assisted programming tools is likely to intensify, potentially leading to increased developer productivity and reduced software development costs.

## LINEYahoo to Begin Corporate Rollout of AI Agent "Agent i" This Summer

LINEYahoo has begun deployment of the **AI agent "Agent i,"** centered around the concept of "AI that anyone can use, every day." This service will be offered for both personal and corporate use, with full-scale corporate rollout beginning this summer.

Features include 24-hour AI customer service, as well as a **complimentary customer acquisition and analytics agent**, targeting a broad customer base from small to large enterprises. Deployment leveraging LINE's extensive user base is anticipated.

## Box Implements Bottom-Up AI Transformation—Emphasizes Importance of Unstructured Data Utilization

Data management company Box points out that **the key to unlocking the value of "unstructured data" (images, videos, text files, etc.), which comprises 90% of enterprise data, lies in AI**. Olivia Notteboom, the company's Chief Operating Officer, emphasizes that AI is a tool that extracts value from vast amounts of previously unutilized data.

Box is promoting bottom-up AI transformation using a "four-quadrant priority visualization matrix" and a "five-stage maturity model," seeking to fundamentally transform corporate data utilization strategies.

**Impact on Our Lives**: The effective utilization of vast unstructured data held by enterprises could result in higher-quality services and decision-making.

## Unauthorized Access Reported to Anthropic's Cybersecurity Tool "Mythos"

A report indicates that **an unauthorized group has gained access** to "Mythos," a proprietary cybersecurity tool developed by AI safety company Anthropic. Anthropic is investigating these claims and states that no actual system damage has been confirmed at this time.

This incident suggests that AI companies' security systems are facing sophisticated threats and may prompt stronger security standards across the industry.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Strengthened security at AI companies will make trust in AI technology safety increasingly important.
