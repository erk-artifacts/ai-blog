## Elon Musk Sues Sam Altman Over OpenAI's Commercialization, Major Lawsuit Begins

A lawsuit filed by Elon Musk, co-founder of U.S.-based OpenAI, against CEO Sam Altman and OpenAI has begun in earnest. Musk argues that "the promise of operating as a non-profit organization was broken" and is seeking up to $134 billion in damages and Altman's removal from office.

The focus of the lawsuit is OpenAI's transformation into a for-profit enterprise. Musk claims that when the company was founded in 2015, there was a promise to conduct AI research as a non-profit to protect humanity's safety. Meanwhile, OpenAI dismisses Musk's claims as "jealous interference."

The trial has attracted significant attention, and its verdict could have major implications for OpenAI's planned IPO (initial public offering) and its current $850 billion company valuation. On the witness stand, Musk stated, "It's all to save humanity," emphasizing his position that OpenAI's commercialization goes against humanity's future.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Depending on the lawsuit's outcome, OpenAI's management policies and direction of AI development could change significantly.

## Anthropic Integrates Claude with Blender, Creative Work AI Transformation Accelerates

AI company Anthropic has announced a project to integrate its LLM (large language model) "Claude" with 3D creation tool "Blender." Simultaneously, it has stated that it will participate as a top-tier supporter in the Blender Development Fund.

This integration enables Claude to operate Blender through natural language commands from within the application. For example, instructions like "rotate the 3D model 45 degrees to the left" can be executed simply by typing them as text. A Blender connector based on MCP (Model Context Protocol) has been developed to significantly accelerate AI adoption in production environments.

Anthropic plans to expand this initiative to products from Adobe and Autodesk as well. AI adoption across the entire digital content creation industry is progressing rapidly.

**Impact on Our Lives:** In creative industries such as film, gaming, and design, AI-driven automation is advancing, promising shorter production times and improved quality.

## AWS Begins Offering OpenAI's Latest AI Models Through Amazon Bedrock

Amazon Web Services (AWS) CEO Matt Garman announced that OpenAI's latest AI models will be offered through the generative AI service "Amazon Bedrock."

CEO Garman commented that this "will unlock tremendous possibilities," emphasizing the importance of AWS users having direct access to OpenAI's cutting-edge technology. Amazon Bedrock is a platform that integrates models from multiple AI companies, and with this OpenAI integration, enterprise users can now choose from a wider range of options to select the most suitable AI for their needs.

This move demonstrates a strategic partnership between AWS and OpenAI, signifying further acceleration in the convergence of cloud computing and generative AI.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Companies will have significantly expanded choices and flexibility when adopting AI, and the diversification of services leveraging AI will advance.

## Anthropic's New LLM "Claude Mythos" Discovers Thousands of Vulnerabilities, Sends Shockwaves Through Security Industry

Anthropic's announcement of a new LLM, "Claude Mythos Preview," is creating ripples across the industry. This model has demonstrated the ability to discover thousands of vulnerabilities (security flaws) that were previously overlooked by conventional AI.

Vulnerability discovery is extremely crucial on the front lines of cybersecurity. Claude Mythos's ability to identify such a large number of vulnerabilities suggests that AI may possess more advanced inspection capabilities than traditional security analysis tools, potentially having a major impact on the entire security industry.

However, concerns have also been raised from the industry about the risk of such powerful security analysis capabilities being misused.

**Impact on Our Lives:** As AI-driven vulnerability discovery advances, defenses against cyberattacks will become more robust, and the security of digital services is expected to improve.
