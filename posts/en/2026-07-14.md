## Fujitsu Accelerates System Modernization with AI!

Fujitsu has launched the "Fujitsu AI-driven Modernization Service." This service migrates old legacy systems to modern environments, leveraging AI (Artificial Intelligence) to automate system evaluation, source code conversion, and testing. This can shorten project timelines by up to 40%. AI is expected to play a significant role in resolving the IT debt faced by companies.

Impact on Our Lives: As corporate systems are efficiently modernized, there is potential for improved service quality and cost reduction.
