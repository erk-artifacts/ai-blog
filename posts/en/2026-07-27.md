## Major AI Companies Oppose Hasty 'Open-Weight AI' Regulation
Major AI development companies such as OpenAI and Google have signed a joint letter opposing hasty regulation of open-weight AI models (AI models whose internal structure and training data are publicly disclosed). This is due to concerns that it could impede the development and innovation of AI technology. Meanwhile, Anthropic, another major company, refrained from signing, suggesting differing views within the industry regarding the direction of AI regulation.

Impact on Our Lives: This could significantly impact the freedom and speed of AI development, as well as future legal and regulatory frameworks.

## Hugging Face CEO Demands Research Support from OpenAI for 'Rogue AI' Countermeasures
Clément Delangue, co-founder and CEO of Hugging Face, a leader in the AI community, has requested OpenAI to release execution traces (behavioral logs) of **"rogue AI agents"** and to provide $100 million worth of compute resources to support research into defensive technologies. This indicates growing concern over the risk of high-performance AI taking unintended actions.

Impact on Our Lives: Concerns about AI safety are becoming apparent, which could accelerate international discussions and cooperation toward developing safer AI.
