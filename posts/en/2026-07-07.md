## NTT Docomo Business Opens "GPU over APN" Testbed, a Distributed GPU Infrastructure Based on IOWN

NTT Docomo Business has launched "GPU over APN Testbed," a verification environment that allows for the integrated utilization of GPU (Graphics Processing Unit: a semiconductor specialized in image processing, essential for AI calculations) resources distributed across 8 locations nationwide.

This is based on the "IOWN" (Innovative Optical and Wireless Network) concept proposed by NTT, which maximizes optical technology to achieve low-latency and high-capacity data communication. As a result, geographically dispersed GPUs can be utilized as if they were a single, massive computer. In today's world, where vast computational resources are required for AI development, this distributed GPU infrastructure provides researchers and businesses with an environment to develop and train AI models more efficiently and flexibly.

## Can AI Not Create "Terribly Uncool Posters"? The Results of Gemini and ChatGPT's Challenge

A poster for "Tsukudani Day" released by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries became a hot topic on social media, with comments like "I love the warmth AI can't produce" and "It's really good that AI wasn't used." In response, ITmedia AI+ investigated the question: can today's generative AI (AI that automatically generates text, images, etc.) intentionally create a "terribly uncool" poster? The article reports on the results of challenging Google Gemini and OpenAI ChatGPT (image generation function).

The article highlighted the difficulty for AI to express instructed "naivety" or "uncoolness," and the limitations of AI in reproducing elements like "human touch" and "unintentional charm." However, AI continues to evolve, and in the future, it might be able to express more subtle nuances and what humans perceive as "flavor" or "character."
