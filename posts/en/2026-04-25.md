## Google Invests Up to $40 Billion in Anthropic, Accelerating Capital Concentration Among AI Giants

Google announced an investment of up to $40 billion (approximately 4.2 trillion yen) in AI company Anthropic. The investment includes both cash and compute resources (computational capacity). Amazon has also recently announced its own investment in Anthropic, further accelerating capital concentration in large-scale LLM (large language model) development companies.

This movement demonstrates that AI companies require massive computational resources and capital to develop and operate high-performance models, symbolizing increasing capital intensity within the industry.

[→ Read original article (TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/google-to-invest-up-to-40b-in-anthropic-in-cash-and-compute/)

**Impact on Our Lives:** AI services under Google will become more powerful, potentially improving everyday AI functions such as search and image generation.

## Meta Partners with AWS, Adopting Latest Chip "Graviton5" in Bulk to Strengthen Agent AI

Meta announced a partnership with Amazon Web Services (AWS) to strengthen agent AI (AI that autonomously executes tasks). The company is introducing tens of millions of AWS Graviton5 (the latest Arm-based processors) to optimize CPU-intensive processing for inference, code generation, and autonomous task orchestration.

This partnership demonstrates a strategy in which AI companies diversify their infrastructure by collaborating with multiple providers rather than depending on a single vendor, simultaneously realizing AI infrastructure diversification and energy efficiency improvements. Meta is rapidly strengthening the foundation for next-generation AI development.

[→ Read original article (ITmedia)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2604/25/news024.html)

**Impact on Our Lives:** Meta's security and recommendation features will become more efficient, and VR and metaverse experiences will become more refined.

## DeepSeek Announces V4, Significantly Enhancing Long-Text Processing Capability

Chinese AI company DeepSeek unveiled a preview of its new flagship model "V4." The major feature of V4 is that it can process **significantly longer prompts (input text) than previous-generation models**. The new design enables more efficient processing of large volumes of text.

By providing open-source LLMs (large language models) free of charge, DeepSeek dominates US competitors in terms of pricing and has gained support from developers and countries seeking AI sovereignty (technological independence).

[→ Read original article (MIT Technology Review)](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/04/24/1136422/why-deepseeks-v4-matters/)

**Impact on Our Lives:** As open AI models become more powerful, individuals and small enterprises will be able to access advanced AI functionality at low cost.

## ComfyUI Reaches $500 Million Valuation, Creator-Focused AI Generation Tool on the Rise

AI image, video, and audio generation tool "ComfyUI," which enables creators to maintain fine-grained control over AI-generated media, has reached a company valuation of $500 million. The company has also completed $30 million in funding.

This tool addresses the demand to **maintain creators' creativity and adjustment capabilities while using generative AI**, functioning not merely as an automation tool but as a platform enabling **human-AI collaboration**.

[→ Read original article (TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/comfyui-hits-500m-valuation-as-creators-seek-more-control-over-ai-generated-media/)

**Impact on Our Lives:** Video creators and artists will be able to produce works leveraging AI while maintaining their creative intent.

## Project Maven Promotes Military AI Implementation, Accelerating AI Use in Defense

Through its Project Maven defense AI strategy, the US military acquired the capability to efficiently process over 1,000 targets in a single day during Iran operations. The AI system has accelerated target identification and selection, further intensifying the traditional "shock and awe" approach.

The military domain is one where AI implementation and ethical issues become most evident.

[→ Read original article (The Verge)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/917996/project-maven-military-ai-katrina-manson)

**Impact on Our Lives:** While advances in defense technology relate to civil security, ethical issues surrounding automated attack systems will become a key policy concern going forward.

## Chinese Enterprises Expand Open LLM Strategy, Gaining Support Through AI Sovereignty and Price Competitiveness

Chinese AI companies such as DeepSeek and Alibaba dominate US competitors in pricing by offering high-performance open LLMs (large language models) free of charge. This strategy has gained support from developers and governments pursuing AI sovereignty.

For nations prioritizing technological independence, open-source high-performance AI models offer an attractive option to reduce dependence on US companies.

## Mac mini in Short Supply, Command High Prices as Demand for Local AI Execution Surges

Apple's compact desktop "Mac mini" is sold out, with units trading well above retail price on eBay. This stems from the **rapid increase in users wanting to run AI models locally**. Demand is growing for performing AI processing on personal machines rather than relying on the cloud, and Mac mini's performance and small form factor are seen as ideal for this purpose.

[→ Read original article (TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/mac-mini-price-expensive-ebay-shortage-ai-memory/)

**Impact on Our Lives:** As local AI execution becomes prevalent, privacy protection and low-latency AI usage will become possible, while hardware shortages will drive up prices.

## Summary

April 2026 represents a significant turning point for the AI industry. While **capital and technology consolidation accelerates on a global scale**, **multi-faceted approaches** including **Chinese companies' open LLM strategies and individual creator tools** are unfolding simultaneously.

Google's $40 billion investment in Anthropic and Meta's AWS partnership demonstrate AI infrastructure diversification and scaling, while the rise of DeepSeek and ComfyUI reflects demand for open-source and distributed development models.

A **multi-layered AI ecosystem** is forming in which US-led closed models, Chinese companies' open models, and individual niche players coexist. Going forward, how each company and country navigates this competitive environment will be key to determining the power struggle in the AI era.
