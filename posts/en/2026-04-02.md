## Summary

As of April 2026, multiple significant trends are simultaneously progressing throughout the AI industry. First, major companies like Meta are advancing foundational research and expanding AI application to new domains such as brain science. Second, the reality of safety and ethics discussions lagging behind rapid deployment in fields like health AI has become apparent. Third, Japanese enterprises are accelerating practical AI implementation for security, operational efficiency, and legal automation, demonstrating that AI has become established as a business problem-solving tool.
