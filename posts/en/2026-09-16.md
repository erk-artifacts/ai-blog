The evolution of AI (Artificial Intelligence) is constantly changing our daily lives. Today, let's delve into the latest news and see what's happening on its front lines.

## Meta Announces AI Feature Expansion Subscription "Meta One"

Meta has announced "Meta One," a new subscription plan that spans its major services including Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp. The highlight of this plan is **expanded AI usage**. Available from 949 yen per month, it offers over 50 features, such as AI-powered expression tools and business tools. With its rollout also beginning in Japan, AI holds the potential to further enrich our social media experience.

Impact on our lives: We will be able to use social media more conveniently and creatively, and AI will become a more familiar presence.

## ASUS to Exhibit NVIDIA Rubin-Equipped Liquid-Cooled AI Server for First Time in Japan

ASUS JAPAN has announced that it will exhibit the "XA NR1I-E12R" liquid-cooled 2U server, equipped with NVIDIA's next-generation GPU architecture "Rubin," for the first time in Japan. This announcement will be made at "CTC DISCOVER 2026," held on October 16. NVIDIA's latest technology, which dramatically enhances AI processing capabilities, will finally be introduced to data centers in Japan, and is expected to accelerate AI research and development.

Impact on our lives: More advanced AI services and research will accelerate, potentially increasing the intelligence of the AI we use.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/435/4435168/?rss)

## Google Releases "Gemini 3.8 Live" Voice Model That Doesn't Interrupt Conversation

Google has begun offering "Gemini 3.8 Live," a new AI model for voice interaction, and "Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking," an enhanced premium version with strengthened multi-stage inference. The biggest feature of these models is their ability to **perform tasks and inference in the background** without interrupting the conversation with the user. This enables smoother and more natural dialogue with AI. They are also being rolled out via Workspace and APIs, and safety is a consideration with the embedding of digital watermarks in audio.

Impact on our lives: Conversations with AI assistants will become more natural, leading to a more comfortable user experience.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2609/16/2000001524/)

## Report on Tactical Medicine in the Age of Drone Warfare Published

JISDA (Japan Drone Association) has published a report titled "Tactical Medicine in the Age of Drone Warfare," based on an on-site investigation in Ukraine. This report deeply explores the use of drones (unmanned aerial vehicles) in modern conflicts and the changing nature of tactical medicine as a result. Drones are used in a wide range of applications including reconnaissance, attack, and logistics, and AI and robotics technology are indispensable for their operation. This investigation highlights the role of technology in warfare and the ethical and social discussions that accompany it.

Impact on our lives: The development of drone technology holds the potential for application in areas such as humanitarian aid and disaster relief.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/435/4435174/?rss)

## NVIDIA CEO: No Need for AI Regulation

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has stated, "We don't need AI regulation. Leave safety to us." Huang views AI not as "a new alien-like intelligence" but as "just hardware and software," arguing that each AI product manufacturer can design for safety. This statement is likely to spark active debate both within and outside the industry regarding AI development and societal governance.

Impact on our lives: Discussions on AI safety and ethics will accelerate, potentially influencing the future form and usage of AI.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/we-dont-need-ai-regulation-leave-safety-to-us-nvidias-jensen-huang-says/)

## Postman Launches "Passport" for AI Agent API Access

Postman, a provider of API development platforms, has launched the general availability of "Passport," a new solution designed for the "age of AI agents." Passport enables AI agents to access APIs (Application Programming Interfaces) securely and secretless (without secret information). This is expected to allow AI to integrate more safely and efficiently with various services and data, significantly expanding the scope of AI agent utilization.

Impact on our lives: AI agents will be able to support our lives more safely and smoothly, making it easier to benefit from AI in various aspects of daily life.
