This will allow AI to inherit the expertise of veterans, achieving both operational efficiency and knowledge transfer.

## Elon Musk Loses Lawsuit Against OpenAI

In a lawsuit filed by Elon Musk against OpenAI, the jury delivered an advisory verdict in favor of OpenAI and dismissed Musk's claims. The primary reason was that the lawsuit (legal dispute) was filed too late, and the claims were deemed inadmissible due to "statutes of limitations." This ruling is being watched as a significant event in the AI field.
[→ Read the original article (MIT Technology Review)](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/05/18/1137488/elon-musk-suit-openai-verdict/)
Discussions regarding ethics and business models surrounding AI development will continue, potentially influencing the evolution and societal implementation of AI technology.
