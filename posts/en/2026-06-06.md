This highlights the immense cost of running high-performance AI models.

[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/the-token-bill-comes-due-inside-the-industry-scramble-to-manage-ais-runaway-costs/)

## AirTrunk to Invest $30 Billion for 5GW AI Data Centers in India
Australian data center operator AirTrunk is planning a massive investment of $30 billion (approximately ¥4.6 trillion) to build 5 GW (gigawatts) of large-scale AI data centers in India. This clearly demonstrates the exploding global demand for infrastructure to support AI processing capabilities.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/airtrunk-commits-30b-to-build-5gw-of-ai-data-centers-in-india/)
The expansion of global AI infrastructure will improve the speed and stability of the AI services we can access, enabling new digital experiences.
