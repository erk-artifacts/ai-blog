## China's Z.AI Announces "GLM-5.1" Capable of Continuous 8-Hour Task Execution——AI Automation Reaches New Heights

China's Z.AI announced its next-generation model "GLM-5.1" on April 7, which has acquired the ability to autonomously continue a single task for up to 8 hours. While conventional AI has been limited to task execution of only a few minutes, GLM-5.1 can now **seamlessly handle everything from planning through execution, verification, improvement, and final completion of deliverables**. This brings complete automation of complex business workflows closer to reality.

The emergence of AI with long-duration task execution capability means that knowledge-intensive professions such as data analysis, software development, and content creation can now achieve complete automation based on human instruction.

## Anthropic Postpones Public Release of "Claude Mythos"——Cautious Approach Due to Exceptionally High Performance

Anthropic formally announced its frontier model "Claude Mythos Preview" on April 7. However, surprisingly, **public release of this model has been postponed**. As competition in LLM (Large Language Model) development intensifies, Anthropic is prioritizing **safety and responsible deployment** over performance. This sends an important signal to the entire industry.

## Money Forward "AI Cowork" Set for July Launch——Automating Back Office Operations with Natural Language Instructions

Money Forward announced on April 7 that it will begin offering "Money Forward AI Cowork" in July, a service where **AI agents** autonomously handle back office operations including accounting, human resources, and legal matters.

The key feature is that multiple AI agents **operate in parallel following ambiguous natural language instructions** to complete tasks. While previously clear single tasks like "issue an invoice" were required, now vague instructions such as "process all this month's accounting work" enable seamless automation from billing management through settlement processing. The service targets Money Forward Cloud users and requires no MCP (Model Context Protocol) configuration.

This marks the first major step toward full-scale introduction of AI agents to back office operations in Japanese small and medium-sized enterprises.

[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2604/08/news051.html)

**Impact on Our Lives:** Administrative burden on SMEs will be significantly reduced, allowing management to spend more time on strategic business tasks.

## SoftBank Group "Physical AI" Strategy——Strengthening Robotics Through ABB Acquisition

SoftBank Group (SBG) is significantly strengthening its AI robot business. Following the announcement of its acquisition of ABB's robotics division in October 2025, efforts to realize **"Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)"** are accelerating.

Masayoshi Son, representative director, chairman and president of SBG, has stated: "SoftBank Group's next frontier is 'Physical AI.'" A strategy is being deployed where the combination of communication infrastructure and robotics brings "intelligence and speed" to robots through communications.

Rather than mere robot manufacturing, the goal is **AI in physical space through the fusion of 5G/6G and other communication technologies with AI**, with deployment expected across a wide range of sectors from industrial robots to humanoid robots.

## Arm Announces "AGI CPU"——Self-Developed MPU for AI Data Centers

Arm, a SoftBank Group subsidiary, has announced "**Arm AGI CPU**," an IC developed entirely in-house for the first time.

This is a server microprocessor (MPU) targeting AI data centers, jointly developed with U.S. Meta Platforms as its initial customer.

Traditionally, Arm has been a licensee providing processor designs, but is now shifting toward providing self-developed performance-optimized chips. This accelerates **custom chip development tailored to specific AI company needs** and is forming a new AI computing ecosystem less dependent on GPUs.
