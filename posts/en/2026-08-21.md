## macOS ChatGPT Integrates with Apple Messages

OpenAI has released a plugin for macOS ChatGPT that integrates with Apple's Messages app. This allows users to search past conversations, draft messages, and even send them directly within ChatGPT. An approval flow is also in place to prevent accidental sending, addressing safety concerns. This is available for Mac users with Apple Silicon.

## Taiwanese SNS Shares Real-time Information with 'AI Translation'

iwafu Inc., which operates a Japan-bound tourism platform from Taiwan, has released a real-time SNS app called "iwafu Flash." This app specializes in location-based posts and leverages AI translation to share real-time information about what's "current" in an area. This enables foreign tourists visiting Japan and domestic users to understand local events, weather, and crowd conditions across language barriers.

## AI Automatically Analyzes Lease Agreements, Streamlining Real Estate Operations

Paradis Inc. has begun offering "Ai Smart Juusetsu for Rent Roll," a new feature that automatically generates rent rolls (lists of lease terms for properties). With this service, users simply upload documents such as lease agreements, and AI reads key terms to automatically generate a rent roll in just a few minutes. This aims to significantly reduce the labor required for contract analysis, which has been a bottleneck in the real estate industry, and improve operational processing capabilities.
