## Has AI Solved the Millennium Prize Problem "Navier-Stokes Equation"? Also Accusations of Misconduct

OpenAI announced that its internal, unpublished AI model proved the existence of singularities in the "Navier-Stokes equation," one of the Millennium Prize Problems. This is an unsolved problem fundamental to fluid dynamics, and if true, would represent a significant step in AI-driven scientific discovery. However, mathematicians conducting prior research have raised accusations of misconduct, claiming that OpenAI began work after they communicated their research findings, and that the methods are strikingly similar. A controversy has arisen regarding the verification of the results and the circumstances surrounding them.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2609/09/2000001300/)
Impact on Our Lives: This will demonstrate AI's potential to tackle unsolved scientific problems while also re-emphasizing the importance of research ethics.
