## Academy Awards Introduce New Rules Excluding AI-Generated Actors and Scripts

The Academy Awards, Hollywood's pinnacle film festival, has decided to exclude AI-generated actors and scripts from award eligibility.

This decision reflects industry concerns about the rapid penetration of generative AI (technology that automatically generates text, images, and video) into the film industry. It also serves as a warning about the potential for AI to replace creative professions such as acting and screenwriting. However, as AI becomes utilized at every stage of film production, the challenge of defining "what constitutes AI generation" has become increasingly apparent.

Impact on our lives: As AI advances, the societal question of how to protect the human role in art and creative fields is becoming increasingly urgent.
