## Stanford University Conducts First Analysis of Hallucination Amplification Through AI Conversation

A Stanford University research team has released detailed findings on how dialogue with AI (artificial intelligence) amplifies false information and hallucinated responses. This research serves as an important indicator for LLM (large language model) users to obtain trustworthy information.

Through multiple rounds of interaction with AI, a tendency for inaccurate information to become increasingly reinforced has been revealed, and users have been reminded of the importance of consulting multiple information sources rather than relying on a single AI response.

**Impact on Our Lives:** When using AI as an information source, it becomes essential to develop habits of verifying the accuracy of responses through multiple methods.

## OpenAI Discontinues Sora (Video Generation AI)

OpenAI has announced the discontinuation of "Sora," which generates high-quality videos from text. Market demand and business strategy reassessment are cited as reasons, suggesting intensifying competition in the generative AI industry.

This decision comes amid successive large VC investments, with the possibility of significant changes in the competitive landscape of the video generation AI market.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Companies are becoming more selective about generative AI features, and users may face more limited AI functionality in the future.

## Data Center Power Consumption Monitoring Under Consideration in the United States; Growing Interest in Environmental Impact of AI Infrastructure

U.S. senators are proposing to mandate the Energy Information Administration (EIA) to require annual power consumption reporting from data centers. As the AI industry experiences explosive growth, enormous amounts of electricity are becoming necessary, raising concerns about strain on the power grid.

This movement indicates accelerating regulatory momentum regarding the actual environmental and community impacts of AI infrastructure.

**Impact on Our Lives:** With greater transparency in power costs for AI services, users may be encouraged to consider environmental impact when using AI.
