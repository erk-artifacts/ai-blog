This is relevant to users of these Google services and to people concerned about the use of personal data for advertising.

If data is made available to the relevant services through personalized AI features or other means, users may see ads based on their personal data.

Publishers expressed concern that the new terms might permit news websites to be scraped for AI training. The delay highlights the importance of discussions over the terms governing the use of content.

**Who or what is affected**

This is relevant to publishers and news-site operators, Meta business users, and people concerned about the use of data for AI training.

The delay may allow more time for explanation and discussion, but the terms’ content and the future use of content for AI training remain uncertain.

**What remains unclear**

Meta says it is allowing more time to explain the changes, but the materials do not clarify the terms’ specific scope or how the company will address publishers’ concerns.
