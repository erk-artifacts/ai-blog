## AI's Name to "Super Intelligence"? President Trump Proposes
U.S. President Trump announced at the UN General Assembly that he would change the designation of AI (Artificial Intelligence) to "Super Intelligence" (SI). This emphasizes his stance of rejecting international AI governance frameworks and supporting AI growth.
Impact on Our Lives: A change in how AI technology is referred to could also alter public perception and expectations of AI.

## OpenAI Aims for "Failure-Proof AI" by Collaborating with Mathematician Panel
OpenAI announced that it would seek advice from an independent panel of mathematicians, learning from past "failures" related to mathematics, to develop safer AI (Artificial Intelligence). This is a crucial step towards deeply considering AI's impact on human society.
Impact on Our Lives: Increased AI reliability and safety will lead to a future where we can use AI with greater peace of mind.

## OpenAI Releases New Models "GPT-6 Sol" and "GPT-6 Luna," Halves API Fees
OpenAI has released two new models: "GPT-6 Sol" and "GPT-6 Luna." These models employ the same training methods as the flagship "Astra" model while reducing API (Application Programming Interface) usage fees by half compared to the previous generation. They will be progressively rolled out on ChatGPT and Codex.
Impact on Our Lives: Access to higher-performing AI at an affordable price will lower the barrier for AI adoption for developers and businesses.

Impact on Our Lives: High-performance AI becoming more economically accessible will further accelerate the adoption of AI technology.

## Economists Estimate $1 Trillion in AI Investment Requires 2.7x Productivity
While investment in AI data centers is projected to swell to nearly $1.1 trillion by 2027, economists estimate that achieving returns commensurate with this massive investment will require a 2.7-fold increase in productivity. Currently, economic statistics show little evidence of AI-driven productivity gains, making the balance between investment and return a future challenge.
Impact on Our Lives: Attention is focused on how large-scale AI investment will affect our jobs and the economy in the future.

## OpenAI Improves Prompt Caching for "GPT-6," Enhancing Efficiency
OpenAI announced improvements to prompt caching (a feature that temporarily stores the history of instructions given to AI) in its latest model, "GPT-6." This is expected to increase cache hit rates, leading to reduced latency and lower costs. This represents a technical advancement that improves AI response speed and efficiency.
Impact on Our Lives: AI services will become smoother and more affordable, enhancing the user experience.

## Rabbit's AI Agent Now Available Without R1 Device
Rabbit, known for its AI device "R1," announced "OS3," which allows its AI agent functionality to be used without the R1 device. This means Rabbit's AI agent can now be utilized on existing devices such as Windows, Mac, and Linux.
Impact on Our Lives: Users will be able to benefit from AI agents in various environments, free from the constraints of specific hardware.
[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/999094/rabbit-ai-agent-os3)

## Qualcomm Announces New AI-Focused Smartphone Chips
