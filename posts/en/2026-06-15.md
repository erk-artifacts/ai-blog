## Google Sues Organization Abusing Gemini: A Challenge to AI Safety
Google has filed a lawsuit in a U.S. federal court against a China-based cybercrime organization that was using its AI, "Gemini," to conduct phishing scams. This organization created over 1.59 million fake URLs in just five months and sent 2.5 million fraudulent messages in two weeks, reportedly affecting over 100,000 victims. Cases of AI abuse are on the rise, and this is the first time Google has filed a lawsuit specifically due to AI misuse. The move to dismantle the organization, in collaboration with the FBI and three major telecommunications companies, highlights anew the importance of ethical AI use and security assurance.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2606/15/news067.html)
Impact on Our Daily Lives: As AI technology evolves, so does the risk of its misuse, demanding even greater security measures and ethical use from companies providing AI.

## Chemistry Material-Specialized AI 'Cataris' Approaches the Capabilities of GPT-5.5
Cataris, an AI agent specialized in chemical materials, achieved a 79.2% win rate in generating "practical reports" in a third-party comparison with the general-purpose high-performance LLM (Large Language Model) "GPT-5.5." This demonstrates the potential for specialized AI in specific fields to generate expert-level output comparable to, or even surpassing, general-purpose high-performance AI models. The era has arrived where specialized AI exerts significant power in fields requiring expert knowledge.
