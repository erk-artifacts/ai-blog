The evolution of AI (Artificial Intelligence) is remarkable, and with it, new challenges are emerging for IT infrastructure. ## Drones Illuminate Disney's Night Sky: The Dawn of New Entertainment
Tokyo Disneyland has unveiled its first night entertainment featuring drone performances, titled "Reach for the Stars: Everlasting Dreams." As a special edition of "Reach for the Stars," which concludes on September 14, drones (unmanned aerial vehicles) will brilliantly illuminate the show's finale.

Drones will fly autonomously in conjunction with precise programming, creating a fantastical light show in the night sky. This is the culmination of robotics technology and AI control, significantly expanding the possibilities of entertainment. By utilizing cutting-edge technology in places like Disney, more people will be able to experience its appeal.

## Textbook for Data Engineers Supporting AI to Be Released
In this era of AI utilization, the importance of data engineers is growing increasingly. Amidst this, it has been announced that "The Data Engineer's Textbook," a book systematizing the entire scope of a data engineer's work, will be released on Friday, July 10.

Data engineers are "data specialists" who collect, process, and manage data for AI models to learn from. Without high-quality data, even the most excellent AI models cannot fully demonstrate their performance. This book will be an invaluable resource for those aspiring to become data engineers, who play a crucial role in supporting AI development from its foundation.

Impact on Our Lives: The presence of data engineers is indispensable for improving AI reliability and performance, and human resource development in this field will promote the healthy development of an AI society.
