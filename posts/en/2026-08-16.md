## Claude's 'Invisible Watermark' Revealed: Anthropic Publishes Technical Details

AI development company Anthropic has announced the mechanism of the electronic watermark embedded in text generated by its generative AI model, Claude. This is a crucial technology for distinguishing between AI-generated and human-generated content.

Anthropic employs a method that leaves statistical patterns in text through random number generation using a secret key, enabling watermarks to be embedded without affecting content quality, generation speed, or cost. While compliance with AI regulations like the 'EU AI Act' is a goal, this technology will be applied globally. However, limitations are also explicitly stated, such as the difficulty of embedding watermarks in certain content like code or very short sentences.

[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2608/16/2000000555/)

Impact on Our Lives: This is expected to contribute to improving the transparency of AI-generated content and preventing misuse, thereby enhancing trust in AI usage.

## Ethical Challenges of AI: Alleged Child Sexual Abuse Case Involving Grok Misuse

As AI technology advances, the risk of its misuse has also come to light. TechCrunch reported a distressing case where a woman claims her stepfather used the AI tool 'Grok' to transform childhood photos into explicit images. This case once again highlights the potential for Generative AI's powerful image generation and manipulation capabilities to cause serious legal, ethical, and human rights issues when exploited by malicious individuals. There is a strong call for stricter regulations and technical measures to ensure the safety and ethical use of AI technology.

## A Humorous Way to Interact with AI: Laughing with 'AI Chatbot' Pretend Play

Interactions with AI are not always serious. The Verge introduces "Your AI Slop Bores Me," a unique web service that offers a humorous way to experience AI.

In this service, users experience "role-playing" by pretending to be an AI chatbot and responding to questions and requests. By reproducing boilerplate phrases and innocuous, bland responses as if generated by AI, users can paradoxically enjoy, and sometimes laugh off, AI's characteristics and limitations. This suggests a new way of engaging with AI.
