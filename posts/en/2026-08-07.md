## Ricoh Japan Aims for 6 Billion Yen in Sales by FY2030 with Sales Support AI
Ricoh Japan, Ricoh's domestic sales subsidiary, has announced it will strengthen its AI-powered sales process transformation support (AX: AI Transformation) by partnering with KnowledgeWork. They aim for sales of 6 billion yen from related services by fiscal year 2030. AI is expected to deeply penetrate sales operations, leading to improved operational efficiency and enhanced customer experience.

Impact on Our Lives: As companies' sales activities become more efficient, we consumers may be able to enjoy more personalized services.

## Ajinomoto's Q1 Performance Hits Record High Driven by Strong Semiconductor Substrate Material for AI and Servers
ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film), an insulating material for high-performance substrates used in AI, high-performance servers, and network equipment, performed strongly, leading to Ajinomoto's first-quarter performance reaching an all-time high. This indicates increasing demand for core materials essential for supporting the evolution of AI and its high-performance hardware.

Impact on Our Lives: By contributing to improved AI processing capabilities, smarter AI services will be provided, enriching our daily experiences.

## Acer Releases Three Models of 16-inch Gaming Laptop 'Nitro V 16 AI' Featuring Ryzen AI 7 450
Acer has introduced the 16-inch gaming laptop 'Nitro V 16 AI,' equipped with AMD's Ryzen AI 7 450 processor. This processor features an integrated NPU (Neural Processing Unit) specialized for AI processing, making it the latest model suitable not only for gaming but also for video editing and AI applications.

Impact on Our Lives: Individuals will be able to utilize AI smoothly on their PCs, making creative tasks and data analysis more accessible.
