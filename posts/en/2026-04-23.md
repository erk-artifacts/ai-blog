## OpenAI and Workspace Agents Usher in Era of Complete Automation

On April 22, OpenAI announced a new feature for ChatGPT called "Workspace agents." This feature is characterized by the ability of **agents** (autonomously operating AI) to automatically progress on tasks such as replying to emails and creating documents, even while users are sleeping or working.

Until now, AI has been limited to single-step dialogues of "prompt→execution," but with the agent feature, it becomes possible to automatically complete tasks requiring multiple steps. There is potential for a dramatic improvement in work efficiency.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Routine daily tasks can now be delegated to AI, allowing humans to focus on more creative work.

## Self-Defense Forces Accelerate Transition to "AI-Centric Warfare"

A new policy proposal from the Liberal Democratic Party regarding AI was revealed on April 22. The Ministry of Defense and Self-Defense Forces are requesting a transition from the traditional "network-centric warfare" to "**AI and data-centric warfare**."

The background includes the U.S. and Israeli military operations against Iran, where AI has been fully deployed for **information analysis** and **target selection**. Japan has determined that it must accelerate its response to this AI era as well. AI utilization in the defense sector has the potential to significantly impact future national security strategies.

**Impact on Our Lives**: While it may lead to improved defense capabilities, national discussion on the ethical issues of AI weapons becomes increasingly important.

## Google Cuts Development Time from "Months to Weeks" with 8th Generation TPU

On April 22, Google announced new processors for cutting-edge AI development: "TPU 8t" (for training) and "TPU 8i" (for inference). The goal is to reduce the time it takes to develop state-of-the-art models from months to weeks.

**TPU** (Tensor Processing Unit) is a specialized processor dedicated to machine learning computation. The 8th generation significantly improves computational performance and efficiency, accelerating development speed for massive AI models such as large language models (LLMs). Availability is planned for the second half of 2026.

**Impact on Our Lives**: With shortened development periods, more powerful AI services will reach the market faster, further accelerating the AI era.

## AI-Generated Image Fraud: Fake Influencer Rakes in Profits

According to a report from U.S. magazine WIRED, a blonde female influencer named "Emilia Heart" who became popular among men supporting President Trump did not actually exist—she was a virtual creation made with **generative AI** by an Indian medical student.

The medical student reportedly sold bikini photos through social media and dismissed purchasers as "super stupid." This incident demonstrates how sophisticated the misuse of generative AI images has become and how easily personal trust can be exploited. The combination of deepfake technology and fraud has the potential to become a larger social problem in the future.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Distinguishing truth from falsehood in information on social media becomes increasingly difficult, making media literacy increasingly important.

## ASUS Announces "World's First AI Processor-Integrated Router"

ASUS announced its latest gaming router "ROG Rapture GT-BE19000AI," equipped with AI functionality. This router features a dedicated built-in AI processor that automatically optimizes network settings.

Supporting Wi-Fi 7 for high-speed communication, it uses **AI-driven automatic network management** to support a wide range of applications from online gaming to streaming and smart homes. By delegating complex network configuration to AI, user experience is greatly enhanced.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Home network management becomes easier, further accelerating the smart home era.

## Corporate AI Adoption Risk: The Danger of "Shadow AI"

A manga series by ITmedia titled "A New Employee Who Will Be Embarrassed by Generative AI in One Week" addresses **AI usage pitfalls** in corporate environments. It depicts a new employee introducing AI tools to work without authorization and inputting confidential information into AI systems—common risks.

For companies, unauthorized AI use by employees can lead to information leaks and legal issues. With the rapid proliferation of generative AI, companies urgently need to establish AI governance frameworks.

**Impact on Our Lives**: AI usage rules within companies may become stricter, potentially increasing restrictions on personal AI utilization.
