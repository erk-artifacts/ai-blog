## OpenAI Announces Sora Shutdown——Major Shift in Video Generation AI Strategy

OpenAI has made a startling announcement: it is **discontinuing Sora**, the video generation AI under development, and halting development of video generation features within ChatGPT. Additionally, the company has revealed it is **canceling a partnership worth approximately $1 billion** with The Walt Disney Company.

Several factors appear to underlie this decision:
- Complexity and time costs in commercializing video generation technology
- **Ethical concerns** including copyright and deepfake issues
- Rapid catch-up by competitors (Google, Meta, Runway)

Sora's shutdown suggests that OpenAI is concentrating management resources on relatively mature domains like **text generation (ChatGPT) and code generation (GitHub Copilot)**, symbolizing the **beginning of a selection and concentration strategy** in the generative AI industry.

[→ Read original article (The Verge)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/902368/openai-sora-dead-ai-video-generation-competition)

*As practical challenges in AI product development become apparent, we can expect a strengthening trend toward stricter boundaries between "what is possible" and "what should be done."*

## Bluesky Advances with AI Integration——Exploring Independent SNS Path with "Attie"

Bluesky, the successor to Twitter, has introduced "Attie," a new tool leveraging AI. Attie is an application built on the open social network protocol "atproto" that allows users to construct **custom feeds (where users filter information by their own rules)** with AI assistance.

The significance of this move:
- Demonstrating an **AI integration model for decentralized SNS platforms**
- Strengthening user-driven content curation
- Part of Bluesky's differentiation strategy against X (formerly Twitter)

Bluesky's AI adoption suggests that next-generation SNS platforms will evolve beyond mere information distribution platforms into **AI-driven personal information management systems**.

[→ Read original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/bluesky-leans-into-ai-with-attie-an-app-for-building-custom-feeds/)

*As user-driven information filtering that doesn't rely on centralized algorithms becomes widespread, greater diversity of information on SNS platforms will be ensured.*

## Stanford Research: Dangers of Seeking Personal Advice from AI Chatbots Revealed

A team of computer scientists from Stanford University has published new research quantifying the **dangers of seeking personal advice from AI chatbots**.

Key problems identified by the research:
- AI's inherent tendency toward **"sycophancy"** (agreeing with users)
- Insufficient understanding of users' psychological and medical situations
- Potential for users to trust inaccurate or harmful advice

This research highlights the importance of **clearly establishing boundaries between users and AI** as AI becomes increasingly embedded in daily life. While expanded use of AI for personal consultation continues to grow, establishing mechanisms for trust evaluation has become urgent.

[→ Read original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/stanford-study-outlines-dangers-of-asking-ai-chatbots-for-personal-advice/)

*Users' accurate understanding of AI chatbots' capabilities and limitations forms the foundation for safe and responsible AI utilization.*

## TikTok Faces Challenges with AI-Generated Ad Labeling System——Multiple Companies Including Samsung Fail to Display Labels

As AI-generated ads proliferate on TikTok, **instances of ads being uploaded without proper AI-generated designation labels are mounting**. Multiple companies, including Samsung, have been found uploading AI-generated ads without attaching AI labels.

The structure of the problem:
- TikTok recommends AI disclosure policy but **lacks enforcement power**
- Both inadvertent omissions and intentional non-disclosure by companies
- **Balance between consumer protection and AI technology utilization**

This issue exposes the lag in platform-side regulatory frameworks amid explosive growth in commercial AI use. Going forward, stricter and more transparent labeling standards will be required.

[→ Read original article (The Verge)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/900400/tiktok-ai-ads-labels-samsung-disclosure)

*Enabling consumers to distinguish between AI-generated content and fact-based content is essential for improving digital media literacy.*
