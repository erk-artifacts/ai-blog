## Copyright Issues Arise from Generative AI: Unauthorized Full-Chorus Generation of Artist Audio

Regarding a song by Yuiko Ohara, the singer who performs the opening theme for the anime 'Mushoku Tensei III,' **malicious copyright infringement using Generative AI** has come to light. Based on a one-chorus (a part of the song) audio track released by the official source, generative AI has unauthorizedly created a full-chorus (the entire song) and disseminated it as if it were performed by the artist herself.

This is a typical case of the abuse of generative AI's 'ability to create new content,' once again highlighting that discussions on copyright and ethics are urgent issues alongside the development of AI technology. The need for legal frameworks and guidelines concerning AI is growing.
