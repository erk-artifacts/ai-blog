## Okta Survey Reveals Trends in Enterprise AI Tool Adoption
U.S.-based Okta, a provider of identity management services, has released interesting survey results on the actual usage of AI tools in businesses. According to this survey, the highest growth rate for enterprise AI tools was observed in *Anthropic*, developer of popular large language models (LLMs) such as 'Claude'. Conversely, *Microsoft 365* (a suite of business applications offered by Microsoft with integrated AI features like Copilot) was found to be used by the highest number of accounts.

This indicates that businesses are strategically leveraging both AI-specific services and services where AI features are integrated into existing business tools. It suggests that AI is no longer just a tool for specific specialists but is increasingly becoming integrated into daily operations.

## Samsung Announces 'Android XR' Smart Glasses Featuring Gemini
Samsung Electronics has announced new 'Android XR'-based smart glasses, co-developed with Google. This device aims to provide users with an innovative experience by natively integrating *Gemini*, Google's high-performance large language model (LLM), and recognizing the surrounding environment through its camera.

Initially, it is expected to be released as audio-only glasses without a display in the fall of 2026. We are seeing 'vision-aware AI,' something out of a sci-fi movie, taking a step closer to our daily lives. This could enable us to access digital information more naturally and further the integration of the real and digital worlds.

Impact on Our Lives: It holds the potential to significantly change how we acquire information and communicate in daily life.

## Livneth Knowledge Inc. Certified as an OpenAI Select Partner
Livneth Knowledge Inc. has been certified as an "OpenAI Select Partner" within the *OpenAI Partner Network* (a program that supports companies providing solutions leveraging OpenAI products). This signifies that OpenAI has officially recognized the company's deep understanding of OpenAI technology and its ability to deliver high-quality AI solutions to customers.

By strengthening collaboration between Japanese companies and OpenAI, a provider of cutting-edge AI technology, the adoption and utilization of AI in Japan are expected to accelerate further. ## Matsuo Institute Awarded Best Paper at International Conference IIAI AAI 2026
*Matsuo Institute*, led by Professor Yutaka Matsuo of the University of Tokyo, received the *Best Paper Award* at the international conference *IIAI AAI 2026* (International Institute of Applied Informatics and Applied Information Systems) for the most outstanding paper.
