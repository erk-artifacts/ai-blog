## SoftBank to Invest Up to ¥14 Trillion in AI Data Centers in France
SoftBank Group has announced plans to invest up to 75 billion euros (approximately 14 trillion yen) in France to develop 5GW of AI data centers. This marks the company's largest AI infrastructure investment in Europe to date, projecting 3.1GW of capacity provision and the creation of thousands of jobs by 2031. The evolution of AI (Artificial Intelligence) necessitates vast computational resources, and this investment will strongly support future AI development.
[→ Read Original Article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2605/31/news022.html)
Impact on Our Lives: The foundation for more stable and accessible advanced AI services will be strengthened.

## GitHub Copilot's New Billing Model Causes a Stir Among Developers
GitHub Copilot, an AI-powered programming assistance tool, has introduced a new token-based billing method, sparking debate among developers. This shift from a time-based model to one that charges based on the amount of code used (tokens) could significantly impact developers' cost management. While AI-generated code is becoming commonplace, its economic aspects are also gaining importance.
[→ Read Original Article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/30/what-a-joke-github-copilots-new-token-based-billing-spurs-consternation-among-devs/)
Impact on Our Lives: Fluctuations in development costs could indirectly affect software development speed and pricing.
