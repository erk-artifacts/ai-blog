## SpaceX Achieves Remarkable Growth with Massive Investment in AI Business

SpaceX announced its Q2 2026 financial results, the first since its IPO (Initial Public Offering), revealing a 92% year-over-year increase in revenue, reaching $7.814 billion. Particularly noteworthy is the AI business, which, following its merger with Elon Musk's AI company xAI, is driving this rapid growth with a 247% increase in revenue. The company has invested a massive $18.369 billion in capital expenditure for AI infrastructure, and CEO Musk stated that annual revenue is expected to reach $1 trillion by 2030. This suggests that large-scale strategic investments in the AI sector are accelerating the company's growth.

## AI Supports Special Needs Education Plan Creation with 'Sukusuku' New Feature

Sukusuku, an integrated support cloud used in special needs education settings, has launched a new feature that assists with plan creation through AI (Artificial Intelligence) dialogue. This functionality allows teachers and support staff to efficiently create individualized education support plans and instructional plans by interacting with AI. It is expected to reduce the burden of creating plans that require specialized knowledge, enabling educators to focus on providing higher-quality individualized support.
