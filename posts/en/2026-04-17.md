## OpenAI Announces "GPT-Rosalind," a Reasoning Model Specialized in Life Sciences

OpenAI has announced "GPT-Rosalind," a new reasoning model specialized in life sciences. Developed to accelerate drug discovery and genomics research, this model integrates with over 50 scientific tools.

Named after scientist Rosalind Franklin, who contributed to the elucidation of DNA structure, this model is limited to qualified organizations that have passed rigorous review processes with safety considerations in mind. This announcement represents **a new milestone in AI utilization in the life sciences field**.

*Impact on our lives*: The shortened development period for new drugs could make treatments for intractable diseases more accessible to us.
