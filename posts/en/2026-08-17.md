## AI Recruitment Assessment 'PitA' Streamlines Corporate Hiring
Scale Technology Inc. has officially released "PitA," an AI recruitment assessment for small and medium-sized businesses. Through a scenario-based test lasting approximately 15-20 minutes, AI analyzes and visualizes innate personality traits and acquired behavioral characteristics. It is based on the Big Five personality theory in psychology (a model that classifies human personality into five factors) and contributes to reducing recruitment mismatches.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/425/4425577/?rss)
Corporate recruitment activities will become more efficient and objective, potentially making it easier for job seekers to find suitable workplaces.

## Software Development Evolving with AI Agents: 'Spec-Driven Development'
Spec-Driven Development is a new development methodology where, before instructing AI (Artificial Intelligence) agents for software development, "specifications" (detailed requirements such as what functions to include) are created in natural language, and code implementation and testing proceed based on those. With AI deeply involved in the development process, more efficient and high-quality software development is anticipated.
[→ Read the original article (Nikkei xTECH)](https://xtech.nikkei.com/atcl/nxt/mag/nc/18/020600009/080700229/)
Software development will become faster and more accurate, accelerating the evolution of digital services and applications we use.
