## Chinese Humanoid Robot Breaks Human Half-Marathon World Record

A humanoid robot crossed the finish line in 50 minutes and 26 seconds at a half-marathon held in Beijing, China. This significantly surpassed the men's human world record of 57 minutes and 20 seconds. This represents a reduction of just 1 hour and 50 minutes from last year.

China has positioned 2026 as the "commercialization year" for humanoid robots, and this achievement demonstrates its technological capabilities to both domestic and international audiences. It is clear evidence of the rapid progress in practical applications of robotics.

[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2604/20/news057.html)

**Impact on Our Lives:** The performance improvements of humanoid robots could accelerate the widespread adoption of robots that supplement human labor in logistics, manufacturing, and hazardous work in the future.

## AI Agent Usage Surges in China, Emerging as "24/7 Digital Secretary"

Usage of AI agents—AI systems that autonomously perform various tasks on personal computers—has surged dramatically in China since the beginning of this year. Because these systems can complete business tasks without human intervention, such as auto-replying to emails, they are increasingly valued as "personal secretaries that work 24/7."

While not only IT giants but also local governments are promoting their adoption, security issues such as personal information leaks have simultaneously emerged. Government agencies are strengthening their vigilance against information leakage risks.

[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2604/20/news056.html)

**Impact on Our Lives:** As AI agents become more widespread, administrative task automation will progress, freeing humans to focus on more creative work, while the importance of privacy protection will become increasingly paramount.

## Summary

The cutting edge of AI in 2026 is characterized by a clear **transition into the implementation phase**. From China's humanoid robot technology to the surge in AI agents, AI avatar-driven business automation, and the commercialization of human flow analysis AI—AI is rapidly shifting from the research stage to practical business solutions.

Particularly noteworthy is the fact that **AI is being simultaneously implemented across multiple industry sectors**. Across all sectors—manufacturing, services, sales activities—AI technology is now functioning as an element that provides competitive advantage.

However, as demonstrated by the rapid expansion of AI agents in China, information security and privacy protection challenges must be addressed in parallel. Future growth in the AI industry will depend on balancing technological innovation with reliability assurance.
