## Mistral AI Secures $830 Million in Funding for Europe's Largest-Scale AI Infrastructure

French AI company Mistral AI announced it has secured $830 million in funding. These funds will be allocated to operating data centers near Paris, serving as infrastructure for training and inference of **LLMs (Large Language Models)**.

The company will adopt the latest GPUs such as NVIDIA H200 and aims to build 200 megawatts of capacity across Europe by the end of 2027. This initiative is aimed at securing AI sovereignty within the EU and reducing technological dependence on the United States. Japan's Mitsubishi UFJ Bank is also participating in funding, with international investment concentrated in the project.

**Impact on Our Lives:** With an enriched lineup of European-made AI models, EU users who prioritize privacy and data regulations may gain access to safer and more trustworthy AI services.

## Adobe Significantly Advances AI Features Across Firefly, Acrobat, and Other Products

Through a briefing session, Adobe shared the latest progress in its AI strategy across each product under its new mission "Empowering Everyone to Create."

This includes strengthening the model strategy for **Firefly** (image generation AI), implementing auto-generation features for presentation materials in Acrobat using AI, and addressing SEO (Search Engine Optimization) considerations. Adobe is pursuing a policy of democratizing the power of AI across a wide range of users, including creators, business professionals, and marketers.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Productivity for people engaged in creative work, such as designers and office workers, will improve significantly, enabling them to create higher-quality outputs in shorter timeframes.

## Mitsubishi Electric and Akari Tackle Factory Automation with Physical AI

As Japan's government growth strategy emphasizes "Physical AI" (AI that operates in the physical world), Mitsubishi Electric is accelerating proof-of-concept (PoC) experiments to achieve robotic motion control and environmental awareness.

In partnership with startup Akari, the company is working toward realizing the "final piece" of factory automation and unmanned operations. Physical AI is not merely a simulation technology but requires machines to make autonomous decisions and operate in actual manufacturing environments, and is expected to be a key driver of Japan's manufacturing industry revival.

**Impact on Our Lives:** As complete automation in manufacturing progresses, production efficiency will improve and Japan's competitiveness in products will strengthen, while the transition of human resources to address changing labor demands presents a challenge.

## LiteLLM Severs Partnership with Delve Following Security Breach

AI gateway startup LiteLLM (a platform that integrates and manages multiple AI services) has ended its partnership with security authentication company Delve.

The certification acquired through Delve had security compliance issues, and the company suffered a **credential-stealing malware** attack last week. This incident serves as a warning to the entire AI industry of how critical security measures are for AI companies.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Security risk awareness when utilizing AI services will increase, and companies will face heightened demands for security investment and transparency.

## Australian Research Team Succeeds in Covert Infrared Communication Using "Negative Luminescence"

An Australian research team has successfully conducted experiments in covert communication by utilizing "negative luminescence," a principle realizable in certain semiconductors, where the communication itself cannot be detected.

Once this technology is commercialized, previously difficult **covert communication at Gbps to Tbps levels** will become possible. This innovative AI-applied technology is expected to have applications across numerous fields including security, defense, and healthcare.

**Impact on Our Lives:** More advanced confidentiality protection will be possible for government and corporate classified communications and medical information transmission, strengthening privacy protection and security.
