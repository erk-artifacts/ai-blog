## AI Safety Measures Could Hinder Cybersecurity Research
Reports indicate that AI "guardrails" (safety measures and constraints to prevent inappropriate behavior) implemented by AI development companies like OpenAI and Anthropic are affecting the work of "offensive cybersecurity researchers" (specialists who identify system vulnerabilities and research attack methods). While AI safety measures to prevent misuse are crucial, a complex challenge is emerging where research activities aimed at finding and improving system weaknesses are being impeded.
