## Amazon and OpenAI Partner on Advertising! Ads Can Now Be Placed in ChatGPT

Amazon Ads has announced a partnership with OpenAI to begin delivering advertisements within **ChatGPT** (a conversational AI service). This collaboration will allow Amazon DSP (Demand-Side Platform) advertisers to place text and image ads at the bottom of ChatGPT's conversation interface. A pilot program will first launch for adult free plan users in the U.S., with OpenAI's system managing ad placements. This partnership is garnering attention as it expands new possibilities for AI monetization models.
Impact on Our Lives: We may encounter more relevant ads as we regularly use ChatGPT.

## Generative AI Workshop for Business Owners Held for First Time in Wakayama

## AI Automated Bookkeeping Service "tofu" Begins Bidirectional API Integration with freee KAIKEI

## AI Autonomously Controls Livestock Barn Environments! Act Node Announces "Physical AI" Concept

Act Node has announced its "**Physical AI**" concept, where **AI** (Artificial Intelligence) autonomously controls livestock barn environments. This innovative system, first unveiled at the Livestock Materials Expo, allows AI to optimize temperature, humidity, and ventilation in real-time, contributing to reduced workload and increased productivity for livestock farmers. It is noteworthy as a concrete example demonstrating the fusion of the physical world and AI.
Impact on Our Lives: Increased efficiency in the livestock sector will lead to a stable food supply and improved quality, positively impacting our dinner tables.
