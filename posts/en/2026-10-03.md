## 2. Apple to tighten macOS Full Disk Access permissions

**Category: Safety and ethics**

**Confirmed facts (as stated in the sources)**

- Apple announced plans to tighten the process for granting macOS “Full Disk Access” permissions. ([\[3\] ITmedia AI\+](<https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2610/03/2000001983/>))
- Apple said it would add new controls so that apps can be granted Full Disk Access only when users explicitly take action. ([\[4\] The Verge AI](<https://www.theverge.com/tech/1004295/apple-limit-mac-disk-access-ai-agents>))
- Apple warned that broad access to files, messages, and other data becomes more dangerous as AI agents grow more capable. ([\[5\] TechCrunch AI](<https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-full-disk-access-controls-due-to-new-risks-from-ai-agents/>))

**Why it matters (editorial perspective)**

Full Disk Access is a permission that allows apps to access a broad range of user files. Apple says that increasingly capable AI agents raise the risks associated with this kind of access.

**Who or what may be affected**

This concerns macOS users and app developers, particularly users of apps that handle files or messages.

**Potential implications (speculation)**

If controls requiring explicit user action are introduced, they could help prevent permissions from being granted unintentionally.

**What remains unclear**

The materials describe the policy but do not give a rollout date or specific steps users will need to take.

Date of the event: Not confirmed in the materials
