## Google I/O 2026: AI Evolves from "Question Answering" to "Task Delegation"

At the recently held Google I/O 2026, a future was unveiled where AI (Artificial Intelligence) evolves from merely answering our questions to becoming "agents" that delegate specific tasks. Numerous features were announced that go beyond simple information retrieval, aiming to make our digital lives more seamless, highlighting how AI will become deeply embedded in daily life and business.

## Google CEO Pichai's Vision for AI's Future: Personal AI Agent "Gemini Spark" Unveiled

Sundar Pichai, CEO of Google, revealed a grand vision to restructure the company's business around AI. At its core is the **personal AI agent "Gemini Spark."** This aims to proactively collect information and execute tasks on behalf of users, with up to $190 billion (approximately 30 trillion yen) planned for AI-related capital investment. The era where AI becomes a powerful personal partner is fast approaching.
[→ Read the original article (Nikkei xTECH)](https://xtech.nikkei.com/atcl/nxt/column/18/00001/11755/)
Impact on Our Lives: Our digital assistants will become smarter, providing powerful support in personal areas, from schedule management and information organization to even daily decision-making.

## Google Announces New AI Model "Gemini Omni" Series: Video Understanding Reaches a New Dimension

At Google I/O 2026, the new AI model **"Gemini Omni" series** was also announced. Particularly noteworthy is its *advanced understanding and generation capabilities for video content*. In addition to existing text and images, AI will be able to deeply analyze video, a complex medium, and generate new content based on its understanding. This marks a significant step towards AI being able to perceive and respond to the real world in more multifaceted ways.

Impact on Our Lives: Video content creation and search will dramatically advance, leading to more personalized video experiences and the utilization of advanced AI-driven video analysis in business and entertainment.

## Cybozu Supports SME DX: Accelerating Operational Efficiency with AI-Equipped kintone

In Japan, Cybozu has launched a campaign offering free access to its cloud service "kintone" as "Serious DX (Digital Transformation) Support" for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Saitama Prefecture. This support also includes AI features, providing SMEs with an opportunity to easily adopt AI and achieve operational efficiency and productivity improvements. It is expected that widespread, community-based AI utilization will boost the entire Japanese economy.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/403/4403007/?rss)
Impact on Our Lives: People working in SMEs will benefit from AI, allowing them to focus on higher-value tasks through automation of routine operations and streamlined data analysis.
