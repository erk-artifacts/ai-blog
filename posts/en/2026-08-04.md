## EU Begins Applying AI Transparency Obligations! High Fines for Violations

The European Commission of the EU (European Union) has begun applying transparency rules under Article 50 of the "EU AI Act (EU Artificial Intelligence Act)," widely recognized as the world's first comprehensive AI regulation law. This imposes several obligations on content generated by AI (Artificial Intelligence).

Specifically, businesses dealing with generative AI (AI that automatically generates text, images, etc.) and deepfakes (fake images or videos created using AI) are obligated to clearly indicate interaction with AI and to affix labels or machine-readable marks to generated content, indicating that it was produced by AI. Should these rules be violated, very high penalties may be imposed, up to 15 million Euros (approximately 2.6 billion Japanese Yen) or 3% of annual worldwide turnover.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Since it will become easier to distinguish between AI-generated information and human-generated information, this will help prevent the spread of fake news and misinformation, leading to the realization of a more trustworthy information society.

## Palantir CEO Criticizes AI Industry as 'Marxist'

Alex Karp, CEO of Palantir, a company that provides data analysis software, issued a stern warning to the AI (Artificial Intelligence) industry, despite having just announced strong quarterly results. He argued that "frontier labs" (organizations conducting cutting-edge research) at the forefront of AI development are so unreliable that they are "unworthy of trust" for businesses, going as far as to describe them as "Marxist."

This statement can be seen as an expression of concern over the lack of transparency in the development process, ethics, and security of AI technology as it rapidly advances. It conveys the message that transparency and trust are indispensable for the safe and responsible development of AI.

**Impact on Our Lives**: As the reliability and ethical aspects of AI technology are questioned, it will lead to the development of safer and fairer AI, creating an environment where we can use AI with confidence.

## Ukrainian Drones Can Now Autonomously Track Targets with AI! Evolving Military AI

Thanks to AI (Artificial Intelligence) technology developed by a U.S. company, Ukraine's inexpensive kamikaze drones (suicide drones) can now autonomously track targets. This significantly enhances the capabilities of drones on the battlefield, with 50,000 Ukrainian-made drones slated to be equipped with this AI feature under a $100 million (approximately 17.5 billion Japanese Yen) contract.

This AI technology could also be applied to future swarm attacks (tactics where numerous drones coordinate to attack), potentially transforming modern warfare. With AI being integrated into weaponry, deeper discussions than ever before will be necessary regarding its ethical aspects and the nature of international regulations.

**Impact on Our Lives**: The evolution of AI in military applications significantly affects the global security environment, making it even more crucial to monitor international developments and deepen ethical discussions.

## EC-Specialized AI Agent "ECPRO BRAIN" Begins Integration with Yahoo! Shopping

EC (e-commerce)-specialized AI (Artificial Intelligence) agent "ECPRO BRAIN" has newly launched integration with Yahoo! Shopping.

This allows for unified management of sales across Japan's "Big 3" e-commerce platforms—Rakuten, Amazon, and now Yahoo! Shopping—which were previously integrated separately. By automatically handling inventory management, price adjustments, and advertising operations, AI enables e-commerce businesses to more efficiently optimize their sales strategies across multiple platforms. This significantly reduces the workload for EC businesses and will be a powerful aid in accelerating business growth.

**Impact on Our Lives**: As EC businesses operate more efficiently, it can lead to a wider selection of products and faster delivery, thereby improving our online shopping experience.
