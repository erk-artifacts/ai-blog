## Claude Introduces Watermark Feature, Met with User Backlash
Anthropic's large language model (LLM) "Claude" has introduced a new feature that embeds watermarks into generated content, drawing criticism from some users. This feature indicates that the text was generated by AI and aims to prevent misuse in professional or academic settings. However, users have expressed concerns about privacy and dissatisfaction with AI-generated content being automatically considered "cheating."
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/some-claude-users-are-mad-that-anthropics-new-watermarks-will-catch-them-cheating-at-their-jobs-classes/)
Impact on Our Lives: While the reliability and transparency of AI-generated content will improve, discussions regarding usage regulations and privacy protection will become even more active.

## ShieldFont Emerges as a New Weapon Against AI Scraping
ShieldFont has emerged as a new endeavor to protect websites from AI data collection (scraping). This specialized font is designed to be readable as normal text by humans but to transform into meaningless information for AI training data. This allows website operators to intentionally degrade the accuracy of AI by making it learn inaccurate data.
[→ Read the original article (Ars Technica AI)](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/new-font-turns-ordinary-webpages-into-nonsense-for-ai-scrapers/)
Impact on Our Lives: Content creators will gain a new means to protect their works from unauthorized AI learning, but discussions regarding AI data collection methods and ethics will deepen.

## SpaceXAI Announces "Grok 4.6," Claiming Performance Comparable to GPT-5.6 Sol Max
SpaceXAI has announced its latest AI model, "Grok 4.6." Building upon its predecessor, Grok 4.5, this new model significantly enhances agent capabilities for multi-step tasks and the ability to generate conversational and visual outputs. The company claims it possesses performance comparable to OpenAI's "GPT-5.6 Sol Max" in overall metrics, indicating intensifying AI competition. The API became available on the same day, with pricing starting at $2 per million input tokens.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2608/13/2000000520/)
Impact on Our Lives: More advanced and versatile AI assistants will become widespread, potentially bringing significant changes to daily work efficiency and creative activities.

## Twitch Content Found to Have Been Used for Amazon AI Training Data for Several Years
It has come to light that user-generated content from the popular streaming platform "Twitch" has been used for years as training data for Amazon's AI models. Twitch has now announced that it will offer users the ability to opt out of this data usage for AI training. This allows users who do not wish for their content to be used to improve future generative AI models to express that intent.
[→ Read the original article (Ars Technica AI)](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/twitch-content-has-trained-amazon-ai-for-years-but-users-can-opt-out-now/)
Impact on Our Lives: Awareness regarding content ownership and privacy on online platforms will increase, and companies will likely place greater emphasis on transparency and user control in AI usage.
