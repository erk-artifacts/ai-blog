## Protecting Elections from AI-Generated Disinformation: Japan Moves to Strengthen Regulations

A draft outline for a bill to mandate "alteration disclaimers" on AI (artificial intelligence)-generated images and videos used as disinformation (fake news) on social networking services (SNS) during election periods has been presented at a cross-party council in Japan. This is a crucial step to prevent the misuse of generative AI technologies such as deepfakes and to maintain the integrity of democratic elections.

The impact on our daily lives will be significant, increasing election trustworthiness and reducing the risk of being misled by AI-generated misinformation.

## 'Shosetsuka ni Naro' Mandates Reporting of AI Usage: A New Relationship Between Creation and AI

Shosetsuka ni Naro, a popular web novel submission site, has announced that it will make it mandatory to report the status of AI (artificial intelligence) usage in the creative process of works. A new setting item, to be established on June 9, will require users to select from four categories based on the degree of AI involvement, and works with high AI usage will also need to disclose this in the keyword section. This move aims to ensure transparency in AI-assisted creative activities and to show consideration for readers and other creators.

Creators will need to think more consciously about collaboration with AI, and readers will find it easier to understand the background of the works.

## Papal Encyclical, AI Involvement in Writing? Fundamental Questions of AI Ethics

An analysis has been released suggesting that parts of a papal encyclical (apostolic letter) concerning the impact of AI (artificial intelligence) on human society, issued by Pope Leo XIV, may have been written by AI. According to an analysis by Lynch Zhang, posted on the LessWrong forum, several paragraphs of the encyclical may have been 40% to 100% written by AI. This ironic situation, where AI potentially speaks about the dangers of AI, prompts deep reflection on AI, ethics, and the role of humans.

Discussions on the ethical challenges posed by AI and the credibility of information sources will deepen further, challenging our information literacy.

This service utilizes advanced facial recognition technology powered by AI (artificial intelligence) to instantly identify users' faces, enabling smoother boarding and alighting.

Our daily commutes will become more convenient and efficient, demonstrating how AI technology is permeating essential infrastructure.

## Summary

Looking back at today's news, it is evident that the societal implementation of AI (artificial intelligence) and the accompanying challenges and countermeasures are progressing rapidly. Globally, movements concerning AI "governance" and "responsibility" are gaining momentum, including the legal regulation of AI disinformation in elections, mandatory reporting of AI usage on creative platforms, and ethical discussions surrounding the potential involvement of AI in the Papal Encyclical.
