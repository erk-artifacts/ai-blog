# OpenAI and ChatGPT Ads Go Into Full-Scale Rollout with Meta Executive Hire

OpenAI has brought on David Dugan, who led Meta's global advertising division, as the head of its advertising solutions team. Dugan positions ChatGPT's advertising model as an "industry-restructuring innovation," aiming for a design that adds value to existing services.

By focusing on ChatGPT's advertising solutions currently in alpha testing, OpenAI is shifting its positioning from a mere AI chatbot company to an advertising platform company.

**Impact on Our Lives**: The advertising experience when using ChatGPT could change significantly in the future.

## Meta Absorbs AI Agent Startup Dreamer—Strengthening Talent for Superintelligence

Meta is bringing on the team from AI agent development startup Dreamer to join "Superintelligence Labs." While structured as a technology licensing arrangement, all staff members are expected to transfer. Dreamer was founded by Hugo Barra, a former Google executive, and this represents Meta's talent reinforcement for achieving "superintelligence for individuals."

AI agents—systems where AI autonomously executes multiple tasks—are drawing attention as next-generation AI technology, with major tech companies competing to invest in the field.

## DNP Makes Vast Manufacturing Drawings and Documents AI-Ready

Dai Nippon Printing (DNP) announced a new solution adopting Oracle Corporation's "Oracle Autonomous AI Database" for its "DNP Document Structuralization AI Service (AI-Ready Data)," which converts document, drawing, and ledger data for AI use.

Manufacturing industries have accumulated vast amounts of drawings and documents, but these have traditionally not been in formats suitable for AI training. This solution transforms legacy data into assets for the AI era.

**Impact on Our Lives**: This could lead to accelerated product development and quality control, resulting in higher-quality products reaching the market more quickly.

## LegalOn Adds Case Analysis Features to Legal-Specialized AI Agent

LegalOn Technologies has begun providing "CLM Report," an analysis feature that visualizes the operational status of legal departments. The feature is provided as a new function in its legal-specialized AI agent "LegalOn," consolidating everything from legal case intake through progress management in a unified matter management system.

This enables legal department efficiency and data-driven operations. It represents an important practical application example of specialized AI agents.

## Nvidia CEO Claims DLSS 5 Is "Not AI-Generated Content"

Nvidia's CEO has explained that the gaming frame rate interpolation technology "DLSS 5" is not merely "AI slop," but rather a properly engineered technology. He noted that game developers have the option not to use it if they are dissatisfied.

DLSS is an image processing technology using AI, representing the cutting edge of AI implementation in the gaming industry.
