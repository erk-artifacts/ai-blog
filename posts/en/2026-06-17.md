## Neo Career Significantly Boosts Efficiency by Introducing AI in Ad Operations!
Neo Career has implemented "Smart AD Report," an AI service that reduces man-hours for ad report creation by 50%. This is expected to significantly improve efficiency in handling approximately 200 ad operations monthly. By having AI automate routine tasks, marketers will be able to focus more on strategic work.
Impact on Our Lives: As companies can operate ads more efficiently, consumers may also have more opportunities to receive personalized information.

## Prox Industries Accelerates Partnership with Universal Robots in Physical AI Research
Prox Industries announced a strategic partnership with Universal Robots to accelerate research towards the industrial implementation of "Physical AI." Physical AI refers to AI that enables robots to learn and adapt in physical environments. Thanks to this partnership, smarter and more flexible robots may soon appear in our industries and daily lives.
Impact on Our Lives: The further utilization of robots in factories and logistics sites could help alleviate labor shortages and improve productivity.

## Microsoft Considers China's DeepSeek AI Model for "Copilot Cowork"
It has been revealed that U.S. Microsoft is considering adopting an AI model based on China's DeepSeek for its new service "Copilot Cowork." The primary aim is to reduce costs for companies using Copilot Cowork. This reflects Microsoft's strategy to enhance the service's price competitiveness and functionality by combining various AI models.
Impact on Our Lives: Easier access to AI services, allowing various businesses and individuals to benefit more from AI, could improve the quality of our work and lives.
