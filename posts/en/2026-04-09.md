# Anthropic Improves Response Speed by 90% with "Managed Agents"——A New Design for Long-Running AI

AI company Anthropic announced on April 9 a new architecture called "Managed Agents" that fundamentally redesigns the architecture for long-running AI agents (autonomous processing systems utilizing LLMs (Large Language Models)).

Traditional AI agent designs integrated three functions—"thinking," "acting," and "recording"—which created complex problems. Anthropic's new architecture separates these functions clearly, reducing unnecessary processing and achieving **up to 90% improvement in response speed**.

> By separating brain, hands, and recording, AI can perform work more efficiently and quickly.

For enterprises deploying AI agents at a practical level, such performance improvements are highly significant. Application across all fields—customer support, data analysis, complex business automation—is becoming realistic.

**Impact on Our Lives**: As AI accelerates, user experience will improve significantly through faster chatbot response times and enhanced customer service quality.

## Google Integrates NotebookLM into Gemini——Project Management Now Complete Within AI Chat

On April 8, Google announced the integration of AI discussion tool "NotebookLM" and chatbot "Gemini."

Previously, when using Gemini, users had to organize documents and files in separate tools before accessing Gemini. The new feature enables **direct access to notebooks from within the Gemini app**, allowing users to manage files, conversation history, and custom instructions in one place.

Workflows for project management, research, and planning that involve organizing information while consulting with AI can now be completed on a single platform, promising improved efficiency and productivity.

## LINE WORKS AiNote Implements Speaker Recognition——Meeting Record Automation Evolves Further

LINE WORKS has released a major update to its AI meeting minutes tool "LINE WORKS AiNote," adding the ability to **automatically identify up to 30 speakers**.

While previous AI meeting minutes tools provided ambiguous records of who said what, the speaker recognition feature now automatically identifies "who said it." Additionally, a "Next Steps" feature automatically extracts and visualizes tasks following the meeting, making the transition from meetings to action plans seamless.

> AI processes meetings completely, allowing participants to focus entirely on the discussion itself.

## Forcesteed Robotics Announces Humanoid Controller "Forcesteed-LEIVOR"——AI Robotics Enters Industrial Sites

Forcesteed Robotics Inc. announced the universal humanoid robot controller "Forcesteed-LEIVOR (Levor)." This controller integrates **AI robotics technologies including imitation learning and inference processing** with traditional "teaching" (manually instructing operations) in the industrial robotics field.

Traditionally, robots required humans to manually teach their movements in detail. With this system, robots can learn from examples and independently infer and execute movements.

> The era of deploying universal humanoids on industrial sites is becoming increasingly real.

## Research Team from Keio, Waseda, and Kyoto Universities——AI Analyzes Cognitive Function Changes in Elderly

Researchers from Keio University, Waseda University, and Kyoto University Graduate School **used machine learning (technology that automatically learns patterns from data) to analyze the impact of retirement on cognitive function across data from over 7,000 individuals**.

Through detailed analysis of individual differences and variations by country—often overlooked by conventional statistical methods—new findings suggest that "the impact of retirement on the brain varies significantly among individuals."

> AI extracts complex patterns from vast data, bringing discoveries that overturn medical taboos.
