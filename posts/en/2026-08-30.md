## OpenAI to End Model Provision for AI Code Editor "Cursor"

OpenAI, a leader in AI development, announced that it will terminate its model provision agreement with AI code editor "Cursor" on November 12. This decision is reportedly due to Cursor's acquisition by SpaceX. OpenAI cited concerns about compliance with its terms of service, given SpaceX CEO Elon Musk's past record of contract breaches. Cursor stated that the impact would be limited but expressed its intent to continue discussions, while another AI company, Anthropic, indicated its ongoing support. This incident highlights the importance of relationships and trust among companies within the AI ecosystem.

Impact on our lives: This will affect the choices and convenience of AI tools in development environments, but it may encourage a shift to alternative AI models and tools.

## Sony Music and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic for Copyright Infringement

Major music industry players Sony Music and Warner Chappell have sued AI company Anthropic for copyright infringement. According to the complaint, Anthropic's LLM (Large Language Model: AI that learns language patterns from vast amounts of text data and generates human-like natural language) allegedly used "tens of thousands" of copyrighted songs without authorization. This lawsuit is sending significant ripples throughout the industry regarding the origin of data used for AI training and the balance of copyright protection.

Impact on our lives: Discussions around the transparency of training data used by generative AI and copyright protection will accelerate, potentially affecting future rules for using AI-generated content and creator compensation models.
