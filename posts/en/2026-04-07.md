## GEN-1 Robotics Model Achieves 99% Reliability, Moving Toward Full-Scale Deployment in Production Environments

Robotics company Generalist's new AI model "GEN-1" is garnering attention as a physical robotics AI that achieves 99% reliability. This model can handle untrained situations and unexpected circumstances, and is capable of handling a wide range of tasks from assembling boxes to repairing vacuum cleaners.

While traditional robotics AI has been limited to specific tasks, GEN-1's flexible response capabilities make practical implementation in actual production environments such as manufacturing and logistics increasingly realistic. This demonstrates that through advances in machine learning (technology that enables computers to learn from historical data), robots can now make more human-like judgments.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Automation in industries facing severe labor shortages will accelerate, potentially leading to improved service quality and cost reduction.

## OpenAI Proposes Industrial Policy for an Era When AI Surpasses Humans

OpenAI released a policy proposal document titled "Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age." This proposal assumes a transition to "superintelligence" (AGI: Artificial General Intelligence) where AI—particularly LLMs (Large Language Models)—exceeds human capabilities, with wealth distribution and risk management as central themes.

The document emphasizes the necessity of methods for distributing economic benefits in the superintelligence era and the need for social risk measures. This indicates that in response to rapid AI progress, institutional design across society as a whole has become urgent, not merely technological development.

**Impact on Our Lives**: Future AI-related laws and policies may determine how they affect human employment and economic inequality.

## AI Agent Monitoring and Analysis Become Critical Issues; Celonis Proposes "Agent Mining"

Process mining vendor Celonis (technology that analyzes business processes) has proposed a new concept called "Agent Mining." In environments where multiple AI agents (independently operating AI systems) work together, this method visualizes the behavior of each agent and confirms whether they operate as intended by the designer.

As an era of AI autonomously executing business tasks arrives, the importance of humans properly monitoring and managing AI operations increases. Agent Mining becomes a new quality control tool that identifies business process inconsistencies and promotes improvements.

**Impact on Our Lives**: The reliability of work performed automatically by AI will improve, and collaboration between AI and humans will become safer and more efficient.
