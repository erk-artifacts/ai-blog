## China Unveils Kimi K3: A Massive AI Model with 2.8 Trillion Parameters
Moonshot AI from China has released "Kimi K3," a massive AI model with a total of 2.8 trillion parameters, as an open-weight model on Hugging Face. It is estimated that running a model of this scale would require high-performance servers costing over 100 million Japanese Yen. This suggests that the AI development race has entered a new phase.

## 'Open Secure AI Alliance' Launched to Safeguard AI Safety
Over 30 companies, including NVIDIA, Microsoft, and SpaceXAI, have established the "Open Secure AI Alliance," an initiative aimed at improving the safety of AI open models and collaboratively developing cybersecurity tools. This alliance aims to prevent the misuse of AI and protect open AI technologies from excessive regulation by leveraging open technologies to fix software vulnerabilities and jointly develop defense tools.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2607/28/news059.html)
Impact on Our Lives: Such international cooperation will promote the healthy development of AI technology and reduce the risk of AI misuse, contributing to a society where we can more confidently benefit from AI.
