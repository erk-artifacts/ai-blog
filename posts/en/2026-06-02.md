## Teenage Girls Using Generative AI for Counseling: The Ripple Effect of "AI's Logical Advice"

Prompted by the arrest of a former professional baseball manager, it has come to light that teenagers are using **generative AI** (artificial intelligence that automatically generates text and images) for counseling and advice. Generative AI is highly trusted, especially among young women, with instances where objective "logical advice" from AI prompts action. It can be seen that AI's neutral perspective is being utilized as a tool for seeking advice while avoiding the complexities of human relationships.

Impact on Our Lives: As generative AI becomes a source of emotional support and a basis for decision-making for people, there will be increased opportunities to consider its ethical aspects and the accuracy of its information sources.

## Robots Streamline Warehouses! Roboware Launches Rental Service

Roboware has launched a spot rental service for shelf-transporting robots. This allows companies to flexibly increase their shipping processing capacity temporarily, such as during peak seasons. These shelf-transporting robots, a type of **AMR** (Autonomous Mobile Robot), automate picking tasks within warehouses, significantly contributing to improved operational efficiency and addressing labor shortages.

Impact on Our Lives: As the logistics industry becomes more efficient, product delivery may become faster and more accurate, potentially reducing the time it takes for goods to reach us.

## "Smafoo," an AI Gourmet App That Finds Preferred Shops Just by Walking Around Town

The beta version of "Smafoo," an **AI-powered recommendation gourmet app** that automatically stores stores matching user preferences just by walking around town, has been released. This app analyzes user behavior patterns, location information, and past activity history using AI to predict potential interests and suggest stores. Unlike traditional search-based gourmet sites, it offers a new gourmet experience that encourages serendipitous discoveries.

Impact on Our Lives: As AI learns our "likes" and creates new, as-yet-unknown encounters, our daily activities will become richer and more personal.

## International Humanoid/Physical AI Hackathon "Humanoid Hack Tokyo" Concludes with Great Success

The international Humanoid/Physical AI Hackathon "Humanoid Hack Tokyo," with over 100 pre-registered participants, was held and concluded with great success. At this event, development using **humanoid robots** (robots with a human-like form) such as the Unitree G1 took place, accelerating research and development in **Physical AI** (AI with embodiment, AI for robots to operate in the physical world). This will be a significant step towards a future where AI operates directly in the real world, not just in virtual space.

Impact on Our Lives: As humanoid robots become prevalent in homes and workplaces, various aspects of our lives, such as caregiving, cleaning, and hazardous tasks, may become more efficient and safer.
