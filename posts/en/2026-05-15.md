## OpenAI Suffers Security Breach! Update macOS App Immediately

OpenAI, a leading AI company, announced that it suffered a supply chain attack that compromised employee devices. Over 170 packages were affected, and there's a possibility that code signing certificates were leaked. Fortunately, no impact on customer data has been confirmed, but certificate updates are being implemented as a precautionary measure. Users of the macOS app are strongly urged to update their app by June 12.

Impact on our daily lives: This reminds us that software updates are essential for ensuring the security of the AI services we use every day.

## Unauthorized Use of Articles by AI: Perplexity and Asahi/Nikkei Face Off in Court

Asahi Shimbun and Nikkei Inc. have sued US AI company Perplexity, alleging that its Generative AI unauthorizedly used their articles from the internet, infringing on copyright. Perplexity is seeking to dismiss the claim, setting the stage for an important trial that will question the nature of copyright when AI generates content. The balance between AI technology development and the protection of creators' rights will continue to be a focal point of discussion.

Impact on our daily lives: The reliability of information generated by AI and the creation of systems that fairly compensate creators will become increasingly important in the future digital society.
[→ Read original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2605/15/news068.html)

## Recruitment Industry Also Evolving with AI! 'RecruitOS' Accelerates Business Efficiency

Pre-registration has begun for "RecruitOS," an AI-powered operating system (OS) for recruitment agents. This system aims to significantly streamline operations by using AI to consolidate multiple tools such as Salesforce (customer relationship management system), Spir (scheduling tool), and WordPress (content management system) into one. By supporting agents' work with AI, higher quality matching is expected.

Impact on our daily lives: Job hunting may become smoother and more efficient with the support of AI.

## The Shadow of AI? 70% of Americans Oppose Data Center Construction

According to a survey released by US polling firm Gallup, over 70% of Americans oppose the construction of AI (Artificial Intelligence) data centers in their residential areas. Reasons for opposition include excessive resource consumption (especially electricity and water) and impacts on utility bills. This highlights the potential for friction between AI development infrastructure and local communities, posing a significant challenge for future AI infrastructure expansion.

Impact on our daily lives: The advancement of AI technology requires us to also consider its social aspects, such as infrastructure load and environmental issues, calling for sustainable development.
