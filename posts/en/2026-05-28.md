## Visualizing the Activities of Diet Members! Generative AI-powered 'Diet Member Map' Garners Attention
A craftsman running a small construction company has independently developed the 'Diet Member Map,' which is generating significant buzz. This groundbreaking website utilizes **Generative AI** (Generative AI: Artificial intelligence that automatically generates text, images, etc.) to display the statements of each member and political developments in an easy-to-understand manner for everyone. This has given birth to a powerful tool for the general public to deepen their understanding of politics and become more actively engaged.

Impact on Our Lives: Access to political information becomes easier, potentially raising citizens' awareness of political participation.

## Amazon Supports AI Video Production! Establishes 'GenAI Creators' Fund'
Amazon, in collaboration with Amazon MGM Studios and AWS (Amazon Web Services: Amazon's cloud computing service), has established a new fund called 'GenAI Creators' Fund.' This fund aims to strongly support the production of video entertainment by providing AI tools and funding for professionals. The production of three anime titles has already been decided and they are scheduled for distribution on Amazon Prime Video. This is noteworthy as an example of AI (Artificial Intelligence) opening up new possibilities in creative fields.

Impact on Our Lives: An increase in new styles of video content utilizing AI will broaden entertainment options.

## Commercial Facility 'Mirai Nagasaki Cocowalk' Enhances Customer Experience with Introduction of Pedestrian Flow Analysis AI 'Misesiru'
The commercial facility 'Mirai Nagasaki Cocowalk' has adopted the **pedestrian flow analysis AI Agent 'Misesiru.'** This AI (Artificial Intelligence) meticulously **visualizes** (making data visible) visitor movements and the impact of nearby events on the facility. This enables the facility to encourage efficient circulation within the premises and formulate more effective customer attraction strategies. This is a concrete example of how AI contributes to optimizing store operations in modern business.

Impact on Our Lives: As AI drives greater store efficiency, we consumers will likely gain more comfortable and appealing shopping experiences.
