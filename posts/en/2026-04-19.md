## AI Chip Startup Cerebras Files for IPO, Expanding Market Valuation

Cerebras, a startup that designs and manufactures AI chips, has filed for an initial public offering (IPO). The company has announced major contracts with Amazon Web Services (AWS) and OpenAI, rapidly increasing its presence in the AI infrastructure market. Under the AWS agreement, Cerebras chips will be used in Amazon's data centers, while the OpenAI contract is reported to be worth around $10 billion.

This symbolizes the growing demand for computational power accompanying rapid AI adoption and has the potential to bring changes to an industry structure centered on general-purpose processors (CPUs) and general-purpose graphics processors (GPUs).

**Impact on our lives:** Improved AI processing performance could lead to cost reduction and acceleration of AI services.

## Tesla's Robotaxi Service Launches in Dallas and Houston

Tesla has announced the launch of its fully autonomous taxi service "Robotaxi" in Dallas and Houston, Texas. Ride-hailing services operated by fully autonomous vehicles without human drivers will now begin full-scale operations in major US cities.

While Tesla has conducted limited test deployments to date, this announcement represents a significant commercial expansion. It marks an important milestone in the transition of autonomous driving technology—particularly visual recognition technology powered by deep learning—to practical implementation.

[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/18/tesla-brings-its-robotaxi-service-to-dallas-and-houston/)

**Impact on our lives:** The proliferation of fully autonomous taxi services could lead to improved transportation safety and automation of driving tasks.

## Video Generation AI "NoLang" Strengthens Auto-Slide Generation Features

Mavericks Corporation's video generation AI "NoLang" has added sample slide functionality for service and IT companies. This feature significantly streamlines the creation of sales presentation materials, seminar slides, and recruitment and training materials.

This is an example of generative AI—technology that automatically generates new content from text and images—being applied in earnest to automate routine business tasks. Through prompt design (instructions for generative AI), customized templates are automatically generated for enterprises, demonstrating the advancing use of AI for business efficiency improvements.

## Anthropic and Trump Administration Relations Show Signs of Improvement

Relations between AI safety research company Anthropic and the US Trump administration have been gradually improving. Despite being designated as a supply chain risk company by the Pentagon a few weeks ago, Anthropic continues to engage in dialogue with senior administration officials.

This reflects the political adjustment process around balancing AI regulation and industry development, suggesting that efforts are underway to reconcile national security concerns with the business continuity of AI companies.

**Impact on our lives:** Greater cooperation between AI companies and government could lead to the establishment of safer and more ethical AI development environments.

## Summary

The AI industry in mid-April 2026 is advancing on multiple important fronts simultaneously. While niche players like Cerebras are seeing rapid value increases with major IPO filings, AI technology's practical and commercial implementation is accelerating through Tesla's robotaxi rollout and NoLang's expanded business applications.

The penetration of AI in the mobile app market and App Store prosperity indicate that consumer-oriented AI utilization is becoming mainstream. Meanwhile, regulation and oversight of AI companies are intensifying, as evidenced by the Pentagon's designation of Anthropic, with efforts underway to balance technological advancement against security and ethical concerns.

Across the industry, rapid growth and commercialization are progressing at various layers from AI infrastructure to application layers. There is growing potential that 2026 will be a turning point for AI industry expansion and stratification.
