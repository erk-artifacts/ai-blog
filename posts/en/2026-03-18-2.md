### 1. What is the Majin-style Prompt?

The "Majin-style Prompt" is a miraculous prompt for Google Slides auto-generation that a person named Majin has shared for free.

The mechanism is very simple: it makes a generative AI (Gemini) output a slide structure proposal in a programming language (JSON format), which is then read by a Google system (GAS: Google Apps Script), resulting in a beautifully designed slide being completed in no time.

---

### 2. What You Need to Prepare

All you need is **Gemini and GAS (Google Apps Script)**. If you have a Google account, anyone can start using it for free right away.

The scary part is that you can create materials of equal or better quality without having to pay thousands of yen per month for a paid AI slide generation tool.

---

### 3. What Kind of Results Can You Actually Get?

For example, using a Gem (custom Gemini configuration) I created, I entered the following rough prompt:

~~~
Please investigate AI hallucination in detail and generate slides.
~~~

Gemini instantly outputs JSON code that becomes the blueprint for the slides. When you paste that code into a site created with GAS and press the "generate button," a slide like the following is auto-generated in just a few seconds!

![Infographic](images/majin-slide-screenshot.png)

Moreover, it doesn't just create the slide framework. It even automatically writes a presentation script in the "Speaker Notes" at the bottom of the slide that can be used as-is during the actual presentation. It's a truly comprehensive system.

### 4. What Makes It So Great?

To be honest, "creating slides from text" is possible with other AI tools as well. However, when it comes to practical use, it's safe to say that "Majin-style Prompt" is the only choice. Here are 3 reasons why:

- **Enterprise-grade design customization is completely flexible**

  With other AI tools, fonts and layouts change each time you generate, resulting in inconsistent materials. Also, in practical use, requests like "we want to use our corporate colors" and "we want to include the company logo" always come up. The Majin-style Prompt (improved version) lets you easily change and fix theme colors, fonts, and header/footer logo images from the settings screen, so materials that comply with your company's brand guidelines are ready in an instant.

- **AI automatically selects the optimal layout**

  In addition to titles, tables of contents, and bullet points, multiple slide patterns are pre-prepared, including process diagrams, merit/demerit comparison tables, and timelines. Gemini reads the content of the input text and automatically selects and places the optimal layout, thinking "a comparison table would look clearer here."

- **Just throw your existing materials at it and a draft is complete**

  Rather than just instructing it from zero, you can have Gemini read PDF documents or Word documents you have on hand and simply instruct it to "summarize this and convert it to slides." A presentation draft is completed in no time. This dramatically reduces the time spent on material creation.
