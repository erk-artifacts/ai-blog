## OpenAI Codex Accelerates Development with Mac Screen Sharing

OpenAI has announced a major update to its AI coding tool, "Codex." This new feature allows Mac application screens to be directly shared with the AI. Developers will be able to collaborate with AI more intuitively and streamline their coding work. This will bring about a next-generation development style where AI not only generates code but also understands and supports the development environment itself.

Impact on our daily lives: Improved development efficiency may lead to new apps and services being released faster.

## NTT Docomo Business Launches New "AI SOC" Service to Identify Threats in 10 Minutes with AI Agents

NTT Docomo Business has launched "AI SOC (Security Operation Center)," a new service that utilizes AI (Artificial Intelligence) agents to identify and address cybersecurity threats in just 10 minutes. This service uses AI to detect anomalies from vast amounts of log data and analyze them automatically, reducing the burden on security personnel and enabling rapid response.

Impact on our daily lives: Enhanced security for businesses and services is expected to keep our personal information and digital assets safer.

## Immigration Services Agency Boosts SNS Analysis, Using AI to Crack Down on Illegal Stay and Employment

The Immigration Services Agency has announced a policy to strengthen cyber patrols that collect and analyze posts on SNS (Social Networking Services) to enhance crackdowns on foreign nationals overstaying their visas or working illegally. In the future, the agency is considering introducing an automated patrolling system using AI (Artificial Intelligence) to efficiently identify information such as recruitment for illegal employment in foreign languages and transactions of forged residence cards, leading to arrests.

Impact on our daily lives: While this enhances law enforcement's surveillance capabilities and contributes to maintaining social safety and order, it may also lead to discussions about the nature of privacy protection.

## New Mazda "CX-5" Adopts Google Voice Recognition to Bolster Domestic Sales

Mazda has announced the new model of its flagship SUV (Sport Utility Vehicle), the "CX-5." This will provide a more comfortable and safer driving experience.

Impact on our daily lives: Car operations will become more intuitive, improving safety and convenience while driving.
