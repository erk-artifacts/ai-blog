## Research Repository ArXiv to Ban Authors for a Year if They Let AI Do All the Work
ArXiv (Archive), a prominent research paper repository, has announced it will introduce a strict measure: a one-year ban on submissions for inappropriate use of AI (Artificial Intelligence) in paper creation, specifically for the act of "dumping" or entirely delegating paper writing to AI. This demonstrates a strong commitment to maintaining the reliability of scientific papers.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/research-repository-arxiv-will-ban-authors-for-a-year-if-they-let-ai-do-all-the-work/)
Impact on Our Lives: The quality and ethics of AI-generated information will be questioned more rigorously, making the ability to judge the reliability of information even more crucial.

## Sony Strengthens Explanation of AI Camera Assistant Functionality
Following some misunderstanding regarding the AI camera assistant in Sony's Xperia 1 XIII, Sony has clarified the functionality. According to the company, this AI does not directly edit photos but rather suggests optimal shooting settings and compositions based on lighting conditions, depth of field, and subject.
[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/tech/932133/sony-xperia-1-xiii-ai-camera-assistant)
Impact on Our Lives: While smartphone camera features become smarter with AI, allowing everyone to easily take higher-quality photos, understanding the extent of AI intervention is also required.

## OpenAI Leadership Change: Co-founder Brockman to Command Product Strategy
It has been reported that Greg Brockman, co-founder of OpenAI, a company at the forefront of AI development, has taken on a role overseeing product strategy. This is part of an executive reshuffle occurring as the company plans to integrate its conversational AI "ChatGPT" and programming-generating AI "Codex," drawing attention to the future direction of product development.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/openai-co-founder-greg-brockman-reportedly-takes-charge-of-product-strategy/)
Impact on Our Lives: As the integration of ChatGPT and Codex progresses, more advanced and versatile AI assistants will emerge, potentially revolutionizing the way we work and create.

## US Authorities to Use AI to Detect Insider Trading in Prediction Markets
It has been reported that the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) is advancing the use of AI (Artificial Intelligence) to detect insider trading (illegal transactions based on non-public information) in prediction markets. By utilizing AI's advanced analytical capabilities, they aim to identify patterns of fraudulent activity that might have previously been overlooked, thereby striving to maintain market fairness.
[→ Read the original article (Ars Technica AI)](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/05/the-us-is-betting-on-ai-to-catch-insider-trading-in-prediction-markets/)
Impact on Our Lives: Increased transparency in financial markets and a reduction in fraudulent activities can lead to fairer economic endeavors.
