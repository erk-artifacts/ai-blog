## NVIDIA's Inference-Optimized Chip "Groq 3 LPU" Delivers 35x AI Performance Boost When Combined with Vera Rubin

NVIDIA has revealed the full details of "Vera Rubin," a next-generation platform for agentic AI (AI that autonomously makes judgments and takes actions). The standout announcement is the new inference-optimized chip "Groq 3 LPU." Designed specifically for inference (the process by which large language models derive answers from their trained knowledge), when combined with Vera Rubin, it delivers up to 35x higher throughput compared to the conventional Blackwell. Seven new chips are entering full production and are scheduled to be provided by partner companies in the latter half of this year.

**Impact on Our Lives:** AI service response times will dramatically accelerate, making real-time AI applications more accessible to everyday users.

## "DLSS 5" Evolves Game Graphics to "Live-Action Quality," NVIDIA Announces

NVIDIA's new rendering technology "DLSS 5" enables AI models to generate photorealistic lighting and material representations in real-time from game screen frames. Operating at up to 4K resolution, it is positioned as the most significant technological innovation since the introduction of real-time ray tracing in 2018. Following its announcement, reactions online have been sharply divided, but implementation across multiple titles is expected to begin this fall.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Gaming experiences will advance significantly, allowing high-quality graphics to be realized even in low-spec environments.

## Back-Office Automation Accelerates as Companies Adopt AI

Japanese enterprises are beginning to move toward unmanned back-office operations through AI adoption. Morinaga Milk Industry is working to automate invoicing processes involving business partners, while TOTO is restructuring business processes and preparing environments where AI agents (AI that autonomously makes decisions and executes tasks) can work more efficiently. Specific implementations are already underway, such as AI handling expense approvals.

From the practices of early adopters, five breakthroughs to unmanned operations have been identified:
- Organizational restructuring
- Optimal workforce allocation
- Management commitment
- Narrowing the distance with AI
- Building reliable technology infrastructure

The notion that "paper-based and manual processes are more trustworthy" has become a thing of the past.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Improved corporate administrative efficiency will enable employees to focus on more creative work.

## AI Adoption in Agriculture: Crop Image Analysis Solution "SWALO" Launches in April

Chloros Inc.'s crop image analysis solution "SWALO" enables customers to execute processes from crop imaging through AI inference to result aggregation and mapping within their own environments. Its distinctive feature is the ability to internalize AI processing that previously relied on cloud services. Launch is scheduled for April 2026.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Agricultural productivity will improve, enabling more stable food supply and cost reduction.

## AI Learns and Automates Browser Operations: Business Automation Tool "Copelf" Released

Colle Inc. has released "Copelf," which learns automation workflows simply by uploading video recordings of browser operations performed by users. Without requiring complex programming knowledge, AI can construct and execute workflows—a revolutionary tool for business automation.

**Impact on Our Lives:** Routine digital tasks will be automated, dramatically improving white-collar productivity.
