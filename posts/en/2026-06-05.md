## Advanced Smart Security! SwitchBot Achieves "Verbal Notifications" with AI-Powered Camera

SwitchBot, known for its smart home devices, has launched the "Outdoor Pan/Tilt Camera 5MP," an AI (Artificial Intelligence)-equipped outdoor pan/tilt camera (a camera with a swivel function capable of wide-angle recording). This camera's distinguishing feature is its ability not just to detect motion, but also for its AI to analyze "what happened" and provide verbal notifications. With 3K (high-resolution) 360° support, it can monitor a wide area clearly.

## AI Enhances Corporate Transparency! 24-Hour "AI Whistleblower Hotline" Launched

NaLaLys Co., Ltd. has begun offering an "AI Whistleblower Hotline" where AI receives internal reports 24/7 and automatically organizes their content based on the 5W1H (Who, What, When, Where, Why, How). This strengthens corporate compliance (adherence to laws and regulations) systems and creates an environment where whistleblowers can anonymously and safely voice their concerns.
