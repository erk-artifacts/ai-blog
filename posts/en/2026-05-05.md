## Expert Witness at OpenAI Trial Expresses Concerns Over AGI Arms Race in Elon Musk Case

In this litigation, the diary of OpenAI's chairman Greg Brockman has emerged as crucial evidence, and suspicions have also surfaced that Musk sent threatening text messages after seeking a settlement.

## Japan's Digital Agency Releases AI Foundation "GENNAI" as Open Source Software

Japan's Digital Agency has released "GENNAI," an AI foundation for government agencies, as open source software (OSS) at no cost. This AI foundation has the potential to significantly transform the "conventional wisdom" of administrative digitalization.

By being released as OSS, municipalities and private companies can now build AI systems utilizing GENNAI, with expectations that this will contribute to promoting AI adoption across Japan.

**Impact on Our Lives:** AI-driven modernization of administrative procedures will improve the quality of services citizens receive and reduce administrative costs.

## Influential Paper Advocating ChatGPT Effectiveness in Education Retracted

This case underscores that verification and ethical rigor are paramount in research concerning the educational application of rapidly evolving AI technology.

**Impact on Our Lives:** A careful, evidence-based approach to AI adoption in schools is reaffirmed as necessary.
