## OpenAI Halts Sora, Key Members Resign in Succession

OpenAI has announced the discontinuation of its video generation tool "Sora" and the resignation of Sora's head Bill Peebles along with multiple executives. As part of a strategic shift to avoid "side quests," the company is concentrating management resources on enterprise AI (business-focused AI).

This demonstrates that OpenAI is shifting from consumer-facing "moonshot projects" to more practical and profitable business AI.

[→ Read the original article (The Verge)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/914463/openai-sora-bill-peebles-kevin-weil-leaving-departing)

**Impact on Our Lives**: OpenAI's shift in AI development priorities will accelerate the enrichment of enterprise AI tools, bringing AI utilization in the workplace closer to everyday reality.

## Anthropic Unveils AI Design Tool "Claude Design"

AI company Anthropic has announced "Claude Design," a new AI design tool built on its latest model "Claude Opus 4.7." This tool can automatically generate prototypes from instructions and files, and through integration with design tool "Canva," allows users to edit generated designs directly.

This will significantly streamline the process from design to implementation through AI, advancing the democratization of prototype creation.

[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2604/18/news023.html)

**Impact on Our Lives**: People without design expertise will be able to create professional prototypes in a short time, substantially lowering barriers to business planning and product development.

## Exclusive Japanese Launch: "Natural AI Phone" Ushers in Human-Centric AI Smartphone Revolution

A new smartphone "Natural AI Phone" exclusively sold in Japan by SoftBank has been announced. This device integrates proprietary AI at the OS level, eliminating the need for users to switch between apps as before. Instead, the device understands users' thought and behavior patterns and automatically performs operations on their behalf.

This represents a fundamental shift in smartphone experience—from the traditional paradigm of "humans adapting to apps" to "AI adapting to users."

**Impact on Our Lives**: Smartphone operation will become more intuitive and efficient, allowing daily information searches and work tasks to be performed stress-free.

## Sam Altman's World Expands Identity Verification Business Through Tinder Partnership

This demonstrates rapid growth in the digital ID market and growing attention to privacy and security technologies.

## Former Prime Minister Kishida Creates Self-Parody Using Deepfake Video on Official Channel

## Summary

April 2026 was a week of simultaneous major transformations in the AI industry and accelerating AI integration into daily life.

**Corporate Strategy Shifts**: While OpenAI halts consumer-facing projects and concentrates on enterprise AI, companies like Anthropic and Cursor are accelerating growth in the B2B sector.

**Rapid Practical Implementation**: AI is beginning to permeate everyday situations, as seen in Dairy Queen's drive-thru AI deployment. Domestically, the "Natural AI Phone" has emerged as a next-generation smartphone, marking the full-scale commencement of AI's private sector utilization.

**Security and Ethics**: Anthropic's cybersecurity AI development and Sam Altman's World expansion signal AI security as a state-level priority. Additionally, former Prime Minister Kishida's deepfake awareness initiative demonstrates the necessity of improving societal AI literacy.

While the AI industry is rapidly maturing, societal discussion about its impacts remains in development stages. In the coming months, careful attention is needed to how balance is struck between regulation and innovation.
