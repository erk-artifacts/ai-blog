## Ferrari Redefines F1 Fan Experience with IBM AI
Ferrari is dramatically evolving the F1 (Formula One) fan experience by leveraging IBM's AI (Artificial Intelligence) technology. The goal is to connect fans and the team in a deeper, more personal way, going beyond mere race spectating. By analyzing vast amounts of data and delivering tailored content and interactions to each fan, AI aims to cultivate more passionate "superfans."

## Has Elon Musk's xAI Changed Its Energy Strategy?
There have been interesting reports that xAI, the AI company led by Elon Musk, is shifting its focus from solar power, which it previously emphasized, to natural gas. Musk once advocated for a "solar-electric economy," but this move may be a result of prioritizing computing resources and cost efficiency for AI models. It's also reported that SpaceX is keen on orbital data centers. Since AI development requires enormous amounts of power, its supply source is a critical issue.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/23/elon-musk-has-given-up-on-solar-power-on-earth/)
Impact on our lives: The energy problems behind large-scale AI development will become apparent, prompting us to consider the sustainability of AI.

## Google Develops "Anything-to-Anything" AI Model
Google is reportedly developing a groundbreaking "anything-to-anything" AI model capable of transforming any form of input (text, images, audio, video, etc.) into any form of output. An article by The Verge showcased demos using Gemini, where a stuffed animal talks or moves within a video, demonstrating its astonishing flexibility and expressive power. This symbolizes the evolution of multimodal AI (AI that can understand and generate multiple different types of data) and holds immense potential in creative fields.
[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/tech/936507/gemini-omni-hands-on-deepfake-ai-video)
Impact on our lives: Barriers to content creation will lower, and our daily lives may be filled with more diverse and personalized AI-generated content.

## All-in-One PC Featuring AMD Ryzen AI 7 350 Appears
At Fujitsu's FMV Store, the all-in-one desktop PC "FMV WF2-L1," equipped with AMD's latest processor "Ryzen AI 7 350," is being offered at an attractive price. The "Ryzen AI" series is characterized by integrating an NPU (Neural Processing Unit: a processor specialized for AI processing) in addition to the CPU (Central Processing Unit) and GPU (Graphics Processing Unit). This enables AI-related tasks such as image recognition and natural language processing to be executed quickly and efficiently on the PC. This move is notable as it promotes the widespread adoption of AI PCs.
