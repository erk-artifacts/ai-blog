## Summary

In April 2026, the AI and robotics industry is facing multiple critical turning points:

- **Robot Evolution in Entertainment:** The arrival of Sony's groovots marks robots' evolution from industrial tools to cultural expression media
- **Diversification of Generative AI Price Tiers:** OpenAI's launch of a $100/month pro plan addresses advanced needs
- **UI/UX Innovation:** Features like Gemini's simulations and miibo's web search significantly enhance user experience
- **Acceleration of Practical Applications:** Real-world use cases are emerging beyond traditional IT domains, from manufacturing to sports broadcasting
- **Emergence of Organizational Challenges:** AI adoption alone is insufficient; maintaining team collaboration and human relationships are becoming critical

AI is entering a mature phase as a technology, and the focus will increasingly shift to "how to integrate AI into organizations and society." This represents a significant turning point for both technology development and human-centered business practices.
