## VAIO's Vision for Future Health Management: Non-Contact AI Sensing on Your PC
VAIO has announced "VAIO Wellness Care," an application that combines a PC's built-in camera with AI sensing technology. This app is the world's first attempt to non-contactually measure a user's health status with a PC camera, without requiring any special wearable devices. It provides "awareness" of daily health conditions, enabling easier health management.

## Huawei Challenges TSMC in AI Infrastructure Semiconductors with "Tau's Law"
Chinese telecommunications giant Huawei is clearly challenging Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), the world's largest semiconductor manufacturer, in the core AI (Artificial Intelligence) infrastructure semiconductor sector. Amid difficulties in acquiring state-of-the-art manufacturing equipment due to U.S. sanctions, Huawei has put forth its unique semiconductor evolution theory, "Tau's Law," aiming for technological innovation through in-house manufacturing.
[→ Read the original article (Nikkei xTECH)](https://xtech.nikkei.com/atcl/nxt/mag/at/18/00145/00003/)
Competition in semiconductor technology dictates the pace of AI technology's evolution. Huawei's challenge could significantly impact the global AI ecosystem.

## Huawei's Autonomous Driving Technology Goes Global: Increasing Presence in the Automotive Market
Chinese telecommunications giant Huawei is increasing its presence in the automotive components market, leveraging its homegrown autonomous driving technology. It has announced its entry into all fundamental vehicle functions – "go," "stop," and "turn" – including electric brakes and steer-by-wire (SBW: technology connecting the steering wheel and tires via electrical signals), strengthening collaborations with automakers.
