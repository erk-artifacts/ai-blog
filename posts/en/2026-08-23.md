## Harvard University Launches Innovative Startup Program with AI Avatar Instructors
At Harvard Business School (HBS)'s "HBS Foundry" program, **AI avatars** provide feedback to participants during startup pitch practices and executive meeting simulations. This is expected to enable participants to receive more practical and personalized guidance, thereby improving the quality and efficiency of education.

Impact on Our Lives: Advanced specialized education becomes more accessible, indicating a future where AI personalizes learning experiences.

## Inherent, Founded by DeepMind Alumni, Outperforms Anthropic and OpenAI with its Research Replication AI 'Faraday'
Inherent, a UK-based AI research institution founded by former DeepMind members, announced **AI agent** 'Faraday,' which surpasses Anthropic and OpenAI's AI in its ability to replicate scientific papers. This technology has the potential to enhance the reproducibility of scientific research and accelerate innovation, indicating a future where AI becomes a powerful "teammate" in research activities.

Impact on Our Lives: With AI assisting complex research activities, the development of science and technology could accelerate, potentially leading to more benefits.

## OpenAI Calls for Stronger AI Safety Bill in California
OpenAI, a leader in AI development, has shown its support for **strengthening** California's AI safety bill "SB 53," a bill it previously opposed. This indicates a growing industry awareness regarding AI safety and governance, marking a significant shift where AI development companies themselves are starting to recognize the need for regulation.

Impact on Our Lives: Stronger regulations on AI safety are expected to lead to the safer integration of AI into society in the future.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/22/openai-says-california-should-strengthen-its-ai-safety-bill/)

## Major AI Labs Yet to Disclose Plans for Containing Rogue Models
According to a new survey, many leading **frontier AI labs** (companies and institutions conducting cutting-edge AI research) have scarcely documented public plans on how they would contain an AI system if it exhibits unexpected or dangerous behavior. This raises concerns about transparency and preparedness regarding AI safety measures.

Impact on Our Lives: If the management of potential AI risks remains opaque, it could lead to social disruption should unexpected problems arise in the future.
