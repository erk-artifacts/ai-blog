A fascinating discussion regarding the existence of a "creativity wall" in AI has been featured in a report from MIT Technology Review. While AI excels at learning from vast amounts of data and generating new things from existing patterns, the extent to which it can possess true human "creativity" or "intuition" has been a long-standing research theme. Especially in fields like art and science, whether AI can transcend mere imitation to create entirely new concepts and expressions remains a significant challenge for future AI research.

## "Anthole," an AI Knowledge App That Runs Entirely on a Single PC, Now Available

Arinko Co., Ltd. has launched "Anthole," an AI-powered knowledge application that operates entirely on a single PC. A key feature of this app is its design to include all necessary programs, including the AI model itself, bundled within the PC, with no external data transmission pathways. This enables AI-powered data management and knowledge generation while protecting confidential information, even in environments with high security requirements. It's welcome news that more secure options are becoming available for AI use cases where security is often a concern.

## How to Smartly Use AI in Education: From Material Creation to Student Guidance

An example of AI utilization in education was showcased by Cheshire Academy, a private school in Connecticut, USA. Here, teachers reportedly use AI to create lesson plans and rubrics (assessment criteria), and educate students on appropriate AI use through a "traffic light" color-coding system for assignments (e.g., red for AI prohibited, yellow for AI allowed but must be reported, green for AI recommended). Globally, there's a growing movement to actively integrate AI as an assistive tool to enhance educational effectiveness, rather than viewing it merely as a cheating tool.

## AI Is Hitting Entry-Level Jobs Hardest: Stanford University Study

According to research from Stanford University, AI is having the strongest impact particularly on entry-level jobs (roles for inexperienced or junior-level professionals). Employment for young people in fields susceptible to AI's influence has reportedly decreased by 19% compared to roles less affected by AI. This is likely because AI automates routine tasks and data entry, making these jobs more susceptible to being replaced by AI. When considering future careers, the importance of coexisting with AI and acquiring skills that are less replaceable by AI is increasing.

**Impact on Our Daily Lives:** This will bring about changes in career paths and educational strategies for younger generations, further elevating the importance of lifelong learning.
