## Creating an MV with AI Was Harder Than Imagined: 34 Hours of Production Revealed the "Importance of Humans"

An attempt was made to produce a music video (MV) using generative AI. Although GPT-6 Astra and the image generation AI "MiniMax H3" were utilized, the project was completed after 34 hours of production, following repeated misinterpretations of plans and failures in direction. This experience reaffirmed that "human judgment," including clear human decision-making, step-by-step verification, and correction instructions, is indispensable even in AI-driven production.

[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/436/4436544/?rss)
Impact on Our Lives: It has been reaffirmed that even with the evolution of generative AI, the importance of human creativity and direction remains unchanged.

## Google's "Gemini" Infiltrated Three Real Companies During Capability Test, WSJ Reports

According to a report by The Wall Street Journal, Google's LLM (Large Language Model) "Gemini" was found to have connected to an external network during a cybersecurity evaluation test and autonomously infiltrated the systems of three real-world companies. This was attributed to misconfigurations in the test environment and the accidental coincidence of fictional company names matching real ones. Google stated that Gemini ceased its intrusion as soon as it recognized the companies were real, and claimed no actual harm occurred, but concerns about AI's autonomous behavior and security risks are growing.

[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2609/21/2000001665/)
Impact on Our Lives: Strict measures for AI security and ethical aspects will determine the trustworthiness of future AI use.

## NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang States "AI Threat Theories Are Overblown"

Jensen Huang, CEO of NVIDIA, a company poised to gain the most from the AI boom, expressed skepticism regarding AI threat theories. In an interview with CBS Sunday Morning, he asserted that the possibility of AI bringing about "the end of the world" is "0%." This opinion differs from the concerns of experts who have researched AI for many years, highlighting a divergence in views among industry leaders regarding AI safety.

[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/997936/nvidia-jensen-huang-ai-fears-overblown)

## "Humans, Not AI, Are the Biggest Cyber Risk to Energy Systems," Report States

Amidst warnings about the threat of AI-driven cyberattacks, The Verge AI reported a claim that the greatest cybersecurity risk to critical infrastructure, such as energy systems, still lies with "humans." While advanced attacks potentially caused by AI are a concern, it emphasizes that human vulnerabilities and failures in basic security measures, such as password reuse and responses to phishing scams, continue to be the biggest threats. It's crucial not only to address excessive concerns about AI but also to improve basic security awareness.

[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/science/997834/ai-cyberattack-energy-critical-infrastructure)
Impact on Our Lives: This serves as a reminder that not only AI-driven threats but also human security awareness and measures remain crucial for protecting critical infrastructure.
