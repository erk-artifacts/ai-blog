## Major Tech Companies Oppose Regulation of AI Open-Weight Models
Over 30 U.S. companies and organizations, including Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Meta, have released a joint letter calling for excessive regulation of AI (artificial intelligence) "open-weight models" to be avoided. Open-weight models refer to AI models whose parameters (weights) are publicly available, allowing anyone to freely use and modify them. These companies argue that open models are the foundation of the AI ecosystem and promote innovation. Anthropic, however, did not sign this letter, suggesting a different approach to AI development. This move indicates a lively debate within the industry regarding the balance between AI safety and openness.
Impact on Our Lives: The direction of AI technology development and the degree of freedom in its use will affect the types and safety of AI services offered in the future.

## AI Data Center Power Issues Exposed: A New Challenge for Stable Supply
A power line failure in Northern Virginia has highlighted the vulnerability of AI (artificial intelligence) data centers. Large-scale data centers that support AI processing capabilities consume enormous amounts of power. Therefore, grid outages can have a severe impact on entire AI services. This incident suggests that the advancement of AI technology is placing new burdens on power infrastructure, and its stable supply will be key to the future growth of the AI industry. For a sustainable AI future, strengthening power infrastructure and efficient energy management are indispensable.
Impact on Our Lives: The importance of power infrastructure that considers the stable supply of AI services and its impact on the global environment is increasing, making this an issue related to energy policy.
