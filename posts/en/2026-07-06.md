## Sharp Aims for Over 80% of New Business Revenue in FY2030 from AI Server Business
Sharp announced its intention to achieve over 80% of its new business annual revenue target of 200 billion to 300 billion yen by FY2030 from AI (Artificial Intelligence) server-related businesses. By strengthening cooperation with its parent company, Hon Hai Precision Industry (Foxconn), and leveraging Foxconn's procurement and manufacturing capabilities, Sharp plans to expand its AI business by handling domestic sales and maintenance services.

[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2607/06/news060.html)
Impact on Our Lives: This will strengthen the infrastructure supporting AI technology evolution domestically, forming a foundation for more AI-powered services and products to become commonplace in our lives.

## Aurum Conducts DeFi Lending with AI Agent for Autonomous Detection of Gold Price Data Anomalies
Aurum has launched DeFi (Decentralized Finance) on-chain lending collateralized by gold RWA (Real World Asset) tokens. Notably, they have developed an AI (Artificial Intelligence) agent that autonomously detects and blocks anomalies in gold price data, enhancing system stability and reliability.

Impact on Our Lives: This could enhance the safety of AI-driven financial transactions, potentially making new types of asset management more reliably accessible.

## CData Connect AI Enables Direct kintone Operation from ChatGPT
By utilizing CData Connect AI, it is now possible to directly operate the business application kintone from ChatGPT (a large language model). This indicates the potential for AI to further promote the integration and utilization of corporate data, significantly improving operational efficiency. Its ease of addition from the app directory is also a key feature.
