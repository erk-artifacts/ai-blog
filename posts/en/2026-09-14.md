## AI Rescues On-Site Labor Shortages! Teachme Biz Automatically Generates Manuals

In workplaces facing severe labor shortages, training new hires and foreign employees unfamiliar with Japanese is a major challenge. Coming to the rescue of such workplaces is Studist's AI manual, "Teachme Biz." This tool automatically generates on-site work manuals from materials like photos and videos using AI. This significantly reduces the burden on trainers and enables efficient talent development.
