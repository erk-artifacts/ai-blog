## ASRock Expands AI Ecosystem at COMPUTEX

PC component manufacturer ASRock announced the expansion of its AI ecosystem (a collective term for hardware, software, and services that enable AI to function) and a 10th-anniversary model for its "Taichi" series, ahead of COMPUTEX 2026, Asia's largest computer trade show held in Taiwan. Of particular note is the emergence of AI-ready mini PCs (small desktop computers). This suggests a trend where AI, previously strongly associated with data centers and specialists, is now permeating into devices closer to us.

## Acer Also Unveils Numerous New AI-Ready Models

Also in preparation for COMPUTEX 2026, Acer held a new product exhibition for the media, unveiling numerous new AI-ready models. ## Fantia Retracts Revision of Guidelines for AI-Generated Content

Creator support platform "Fantia" retracted its revision of content modification and mosaic standards following strong backlash from users. This move highlights the difficulty platforms face in handling AI-generated content (images, texts, etc., created using AI). Abrupt rule changes have a significant impact on creators, suggesting an increasing number of ethical, legal, and creative challenges accompanying the advancement of AI technology.

Impact on Our Lives: With the rise of AI-generated content, the rules of online platforms and the nature of creator rights protection will constantly be called into question.
