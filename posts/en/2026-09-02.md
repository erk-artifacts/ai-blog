## LINE Yahoo Strengthens Personalized Advertising with AI

LINE Yahoo has expanded personalized ad delivery based on user conversation content through its AI agent "Agent i," and has begun charging advertisers. This aims to improve user experience and maximize advertising effectiveness by leveraging AI (Artificial Intelligence) to deeply understand user interests and needs, and propose more relevant information and products.

Impact on Our Lives: Ads tailored more to our preferences may appear on services we use daily, such as LINE, potentially increasing the efficiency of information gathering.

## Dyson Announces AI-Powered "Smart Toothbrush"

From Dyson, an innovative electric toothbrush equipped with AI (Artificial Intelligence), the "Dyson CameraJet Flosser-equipped Electric Toothbrush," has been released. This toothbrush detects interdental spaces with a camera, and AI performs optimal jet cleaning, achieving a cleaning effect similar to dental floss, which is difficult to do manually.

Impact on Our Lives: Daily oral care will become easier and more effective with the power of AI, contributing to our health maintenance.

## Summary

Today's AI forefront is expanding its reach from our daily lives to industries and ethical discussions. Like LINE Yahoo's personalized ads and Dyson's AI toothbrush, AI is evolving as a tool to enhance our convenience and support our health. Furthermore, AI-powered SaaS, such as PKSHA AI SaaS, is expected to support business efficiency behind the scenes and contribute to making society as a whole smarter. However, as the debate surrounding "Physical AI" at the GMO Grand Conference indicates, the rapid advancement of AI prompts us to engage in continuous dialogue and consideration regarding its intended use and ethical aspects. AI holds immense potential to transform the future, but simultaneously with its progress, deeply considering **responsible use** will be the key to building a better future.
