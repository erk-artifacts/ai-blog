## Anthropic Unveils New LLM "Claude Opus 5": Fable 5-Class Performance at Half the Price

Anthropic has released its latest LLM (Large Language Model), "Claude Opus 5." This model reportedly offers performance comparable to the company's high-end model, "Claude Fable 5," but at half the price. It has garnered high praise in programming and knowledge work, featuring parameters to adjust inference depth and an automatic fallback function linked to its safety classifier. It is also reported that the focus is on improving token efficiency rather than a leap in functionality.

**Impact on Our Lives**: With more powerful AI models becoming available at an affordable price, many companies and developers will find it easier to integrate cutting-edge AI technology into their businesses and research.

## AlphaFold AI Improves Gene-Editing Proteins to Enhance Safety

Research results have been announced stating that Google's AI tool "AlphaFold" has been successfully utilized to redesign gene-editing proteins and enhance their safety. This demonstrates the immeasurable potential AI holds in the field of life sciences.

**Impact on Our Lives**: AI in medicine and life sciences could accelerate the development of safer and more precise therapies, potentially revolutionizing the treatment of genetic diseases in the future.
