## Waymo's Self-Driving Taxis Coming to Tokyo!
U.S.-based Waymo, ride-hailing app "GO," and Nihon Kotsu have announced a plan to commercialize an unmanned self-driving taxi service in Tokyo starting in 2027. Rides will be bookable through both the "GO" ride-hailing app and Waymo, with a future goal of operating a fleet of 100 vehicles. This marks a significant first step for AI (Artificial Intelligence)-powered autonomous driving to become a mainstream part of our daily transportation.

## IT Engineers' Use of "Technical Books" in the Generative AI Era
A survey revealed that in the current era of widespread generative AI, approximately 90% of IT engineers responded, "Even with the spread of AI and videos, I can't let go of technical books." The primary reason cited was "information reliability," followed by "ability to reread multiple times." While AI serves as a powerful tool for information gathering, technical books are still highly valued for acquiring deeply reliable knowledge.

Impact on our lives: Even as AI changes how we obtain information, it reaffirms the importance of deeply learning from reliable knowledge sources.
