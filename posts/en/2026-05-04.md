## AI Surpasses Physician Diagnostic Accuracy in Medical Settings

A new Harvard University study has revealed that large language models (LLMs) can provide more accurate diagnoses than two human physicians in actual emergency room cases, with at least one model outperforming them. This research, which validates LLM performance across various medical contexts, represents crucial evidence for the effectiveness of AI implementation in healthcare.

The use of AI in the medical field is expanding beyond improving diagnostic accuracy to enhance physician work efficiency and serve as a diagnostic support tool, with potential to accelerate digitalization in clinical practice moving forward.

## AI Copyright Infringement? Conflict Between Creators and AI Startups

The creator of the popular illustration "This is fine" has pointed out that their work was used without permission by AI startup "Artisan," which advocates "stop hiring humans" in its advertising messages. This is one example of copyright issues surrounding training data use in AI development, intensifying debate over balancing AI industry advancement with creator rights protection.

The copyright issue of content used in AI training is becoming a critical theme with major implications for future regulation and business models, making the establishment of legal frameworks and industry guidelines in various countries an urgent priority.

## Summary

As of May 2026, the AI industry is simultaneously developing across multiple fronts: high precision in medical diagnostics, democratization through no-code development, conflict over copyright issues, and oversupply in music generation.

On the positive side, AI is beginning to deliver real value in clinical settings, and an environment where anyone can develop AI applications is taking shape. Conversely, challenges such as creator rights protection and AI-generated content quality management are becoming apparent, making the balance between regulation and innovation a critical industry concern.

AI technology is undoubtedly permeating society, but interest balancing among various stakeholders that arises during this process will become a crucial factor determining future AI development.
