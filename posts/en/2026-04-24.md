# AI Reshapes Paleontology Through Fossil Analysis

AI image analysis capabilities are bringing innovation to paleontological research. A research team from Hokkaido University and others used AI to analyze fossils and discovered that a giant octopus measuring 19 meters in length occupied the apex of the food chain in Late Cretaceous seas (approximately 100 to 72 million years ago). Traditionally, the apex predators of the sea were thought to be vertebrates (animals with backbones), making this discovery a major departure from conventional scientific wisdom. AI technology has proven that entirely new insights can be extracted from vast amounts of fossil data.

**Impact on our lives:** Accelerated paleontological research may deepen our understanding of Earth's history and provide new insights for addressing environmental change.
