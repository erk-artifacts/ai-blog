## Google Significantly Cuts Price of AI Service "AI Plus"

Google has revised the monthly fee for its premium AI service "AI Plus," cutting it by 40% to 725 yen (excluding tax) per month. Along with this, the storage capacity is also reported to be doubled. Furthermore, price reductions and added benefits are also being introduced for higher-tier plans such as "AI Pro" and "AI Ultra," indicating a move to enhance the price competitiveness across Google's entire AI service lineup. This can be seen as an aggressive pricing strategy aimed at accelerating AI adoption.

Impact on our lives: AI services will become more accessible, likely accelerating their adoption among general users.

## Apple's "Slow and Steady" AI Strategy Gains Attention

While competitors are rapidly advancing AI development, Apple is pursuing a cautious yet steady AI strategy, and this approach is starting to be recognized as a wise one. Up until now, Apple has faced criticism for "falling behind in the AI race," but it appears to be achieving concrete results with its unique direction. It seems Apple aims to fundamentally transform the user experience through deep integration with its products.

## OpenAI Begins Preparations for Initial Public Offering (IPO)

OpenAI, the U.S. company behind ChatGPT and other innovations, announced that it has confidentially submitted a draft S-1 registration statement to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). This is seen as the first step towards an Initial Public Offering (IPO). The company holds the grand vision of "AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) for everyone" and intends to raise further capital through the IPO to accelerate research and development.

## Siri AI to be Included in "watchOS 27" for Apple Watch, with Limited Device Compatibility

Apple has announced "watchOS 27," the next operating system for Apple Watch. This new OS will feature "Siri AI," built upon *Apple Intelligence*, enabling more advanced conversations and web searches. However, it has been revealed that this AI functionality will be limited to Apple Watch Series 9 models and later, with Series 8 and earlier models, as well as the original Ultra, not supported.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)] (https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2606/09/news072.html)
Impact on our lives: The Apple Watch is expected to become smarter, making daily health management and information access more personal and efficient.

## Corporate Governance Issues in the Era of Generative AI

The rapid evolution of Generative AI is prompting companies to reconsider the nature of their governance (management and control systems). Practitioners from various industries, including Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, Toyota Motor Corporation, and Marubeni Corporation, gathered to deeply discuss governance issues for the safe and appropriate use of Generative AI from legal, AI research, HR, and management perspectives. Efforts to maximize AI's benefits while managing its risks are gaining momentum.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)] (https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/409/4409020/?rss)
Impact on our lives: Guidelines for companies to use AI safely and ethically will be established, forming the foundation for more reliable AI services to be provided to society.

## Real Estate AI Virtual Staging Service "Room AI" Enhances Quality

Room AI, an AI virtual staging service garnering attention in the real estate industry, has further enhanced the quality of its generated images by introducing proprietary quality assurance technology. This allows for the high-precision provision of realistic images, making empty properties appear online as if they were furnished and decorated. Additionally, new pricing structures such as invoice payment, 6-month/1-year contracts, and perpetual license plans have been introduced, establishing a system to support serious adoption by corporate clients.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)] (https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/409/4409026/?rss)
Impact on our lives: Online real estate viewings will become even more realistic, streamlining property searches and sales, and enhancing convenience for us consumers.
