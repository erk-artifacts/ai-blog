## Meta Halts Instagram's Controversial AI Feature

Meta has halted the **AI image generation feature based on public account content** that it introduced on Instagram, following strong backlash from users. This feature allowed AI images to be generated from content on public Instagram accounts simply by tagging them, but it drew a torrent of criticism due to privacy and **deepfake** (fake images or videos created using AI) concerns. Meta explained that its "intention was to provide a useful creative tool and allow users to control whether their public content was referenced," but after receiving feedback that "this feature fell short of expectations," it decided to discontinue it.

## Apple Sues OpenAI for "Trade Secret Theft"

A shockwave has hit the two giants of the AI industry. Apple has sued **OpenAI** (the company that develops ChatGPT), its subsidiaries, and two former employees, alleging they **illegally acquired confidential information, including that related to iPhones**. Apple claims there was systematic poaching and information extraction during interviews, and it is seeking damages. This lawsuit is said to be unrelated to Apple's partnership with OpenAI on AI features, but it offers a glimpse into the fierce competition and talent acquisition battles within the AI sector.

## Hugging Face CEO Discusses the Importance of Open-Source AI

The importance of **open-source AI** (AI technology with publicly available source code that anyone can use, modify, and distribute), which drives the democratization of AI development, is once again garnering attention. Clem Delangue, CEO of **Hugging Face** (a platform for sharing AI models and datasets), pointed out that its importance is higher than ever before. Hugging Face has grown into a platform where AI builders can share and download **open models** (publicly released AI models) and **datasets** (collections of data for AI training), with nearly half of Fortune 500 companies now using it. This trend is considered essential for accelerating AI innovation.
