## FIFA World Cup Introduces "3D Avatar Judgment" and "Match Analysis AI" for the First Time

Multiple innovative AI technologies will be implemented at this year's FIFA World Cup. An officiating system using 3D-scanned avatars of players and a match analysis AI tool that all teams can utilize are making their debut.

Supported by Lenovo as the official technology partner, these technologies realized through cooperation with Formula 1 (F1) and FIFA (International Football Federation) will significantly enhance sports transparency and the precision of tactical analysis.

The 3D avatar judgment system will improve the accuracy of decisions such as offside calls, while the AI analysis tool will bring innovation to team strategy planning.

## SIer Industry Shifts Toward "FDE-Type" On-Site Operations Model

The conventional wisdom in systems construction is changing rapidly. If AI is transforming corporate operations, then SIers (Systems Integrators) that systemize these transformations must also change.

A shift is required from the traditional on-site business model measured by "person-months" to an "FDE-type" (Field Development Engineer) model—engineers who advance problem-solving at customer sites. A new skill set is required that understands how to connect operations with AI and practically solves customer problems in their own environment.

The SIer industry is entering a period where personnel strategy and business models are fundamentally changing due to AI proliferation.

## The Real Feelings of Tax Return Users: AI Cannot Be Fully Trusted

Despite convenient AI solutions emerging one after another, survey results show that when it comes to strict procedures like "tax filing," 3 out of 4 people seek "final confirmation by a professional."

While automation by AI brings efficiency, users feel uneasy about completely relying on AI for tasks with legal responsibility such as tax filing. This suggests realistic limitations regarding AI's capabilities and reliability.

Finding the optimal division of roles between AI and humans will become an important issue in future AI adoption.

## Summary

As of mid-April 2026, AI technology implementation is advancing rapidly. AI is beginning to play an active role across all domains—from software development to financial management, sports analysis, and enterprise system construction.

Meanwhile, efforts to explore optimal role division between humans and AI, rather than over-reliance on AI, are also becoming more active. In the future AI society, correctly understanding "what AI can and cannot do" and appropriately applying it depending on the context will be the key to creating maximum value.

Among Japanese enterprises, the adoption of development support AI like Devin is advancing, and transformation in the SIer industry is accelerating. Through these developments, strengthening Japan's digital competitiveness is expected.
