## OpenAI Develops Proprietary AI Chip for Large Language Models

OpenAI, known for ChatGPT and other services, announced that it has co-developed 'Jalapeño,' a proprietary AI chip specializing in inference processing for Large Language Models (LLMs), with Broadcom. This move aims to reduce reliance on existing GPUs (Graphics Processing Units) and lower AI processing costs while increasing efficiency. In-house development of AI chips marks a significant step for OpenAI to accelerate the further evolution and widespread adoption of AI technology.

Impact on Our Lives: The cost of AI services will decrease, increasing the likelihood that faster and more diverse AI functionalities will be integrated into our everyday products and services.

## Acer Announces Gaming Laptops Compatible with Generative AI

Acer has announced its 'Predator Helios' series of gaming laptops, equipped with the latest NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50 series GPUs. These models, combined with Core Ultra processors, not only deliver high-performance gaming experiences but also support demanding tasks such as Generative AI applications and video editing. The names 'Predator Helios 18 AI' and 'Predator Helios Neo 16S AI' particularly highlight the emphasis on AI processing capabilities.

Impact on Our Lives: High-performance AI processing will become readily available on laptops, contributing to increased productivity for creators and researchers.

## Lenovo Announces AI Workstations Featuring AMD Ryzen PRO

ASK Corporation has commenced domestic handling of Lenovo's entry-level workstation, the 'ThinkStation P4.' This workstation features the latest AMD Ryzen PRO 9000 series processors and offers the option to select GPUs from NVIDIA's latest AI GPU architecture, the 'Blackwell' series. This provides high performance and expandability for specialized applications such as AI development, machine learning (ML) training, and inference.
