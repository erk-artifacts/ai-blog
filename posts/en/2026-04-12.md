## OpenAI CEO Sam Altman Reveals Stance Against Critical Reporting

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has published a personal blog post discussing his feelings about critical reporting targeting him and an incident involving a firebomb attack on his home. In the blog, he shows understanding toward societal concerns about AI (artificial intelligence) while warning of the risks that excessive incitement poses in sparking violent acts.

Altman compares the power struggle surrounding AGI (artificial general intelligence) to the "Ring of Power" in "The Lord of the Rings," pointing out the dangers of a small number of companies or nations monopolizing powerful AI technology. He emphasizes the importance of sharing technology widely and managing it through democratic processes.

> "Concerns about AI are understandable, but incitement leads to violence"

This statement suggests the importance of building trust between AI development companies and society.

**Impact on our lives:** Improved communication between AI companies and society could lead to the development of safer and more trusted AI technology.

## Claude Mythos Discovers Thousands of Vulnerabilities, Sends Shockwaves Through Security Industry

US-based Anthropic's newly announced LLM "Claude Mythos Preview" has revealed capabilities that overturn conventional security wisdom. In addition to high performance as a general-purpose LLM (large language model), it possesses an exceptionally high ability to find software vulnerabilities and actually exploit them.

Recognizing the danger of this capability, the company has decided not to make it publicly available and is only offering a limited preview version. The ability to discover thousands of vulnerabilities could pose a significant threat to cybersecurity experts, making the formulation of future AI (artificial intelligence) security policies urgent.

- **Detection capability:** Detection accuracy far exceeding existing vulnerability scanning tools
- **Exploitation capability:** Potential to go beyond mere detection and present actual exploitation methods
- **Release strategy:** Limited public release with security considerations

**Impact on our lives:** As AI enhances vulnerability detection capabilities, the balance between defense against and execution of cyberattacks will shift significantly, making new security measures essential.

## AI Language Models' Soccer Prediction Accuracy Falls Far Short of Expectations

Language models (LLMs) developed by multiple AI companies including Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI have proven to deliver disappointing performance when it comes to predicting soccer matches, particularly in the Premier League. These models, despite possessing advanced natural language processing capabilities, have shown they cannot handle the practical task of sports prediction.

xAI's Grok, in particular, has demonstrated especially low accuracy, with the differences in AI's strengths and weaknesses becoming increasingly clear. This result suggests that LLMs are not universal tools and that specialized learning or fine-tuning (adjustment) for specific domains is important.

[→ Read the original article (Ars Technica AI)](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/04/ai-models-are-terrible-at-betting-on-soccer-especially-xai-grok/)

**Impact on our lives:** When considering AI applications for sports prediction or gambling-related purposes, a cautious approach grounded in understanding AI's limitations is necessary.

## Misuse of AI-Generated Content (AI Slop) Exploited in International Information Warfare

The misuse of AI-generated content (so-called "AI Slop") has come to light in international information campaigns surrounding the Iran situation. While the US government has been distributing Call of Duty meme images and low-quality AI-generated content, Iran's state media has reliably broadcast actual combat footage and had the advantage in information dissemination.

This case demonstrates that the abuse of AI-generated content can paradoxically undermine credibility, and the ethics of AI (artificial intelligence) utilization in information society are increasingly being called into question.

[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/policy/910401/iran-war-propaganda-blackout-lego-ai-slop)

**Impact on our lives:** As AI-generated content is misused in political and diplomatic contexts, improvements in information literacy become increasingly important.
