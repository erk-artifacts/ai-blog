## The Threat of Deepfakes Shakes Society's "Distrust"

With the advancement of AI (Artificial Intelligence) technology, the threat of **deepfakes** (highly realistic fake images and videos created using AI that are almost indistinguishable from real ones) is increasing. Their uses range from explicit images to political propaganda, and it's becoming increasingly difficult to spot them. It is pointed out that technical countermeasures and legal regulations alone have limitations, and particularly with the 2026 US midterm elections approaching, there is a concern that societal "distrust" could escalate. The era has arrived where media literacy to distinguish between real and fake is more crucial than ever.
[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/400/4400195/?rss)
Impact on Our Lives: As it becomes necessary to question whether the information we see daily is authentic, the ability to discern the truth of information will be demanded even more.
