## Google CEO Reveals Future of Gemini and Pixel, Evolution to Agentic AI

At a panel discussion during Google I/O 2026, CEO Sundar Pichai spoke about the future of Gemini, the company's foundational AI model. He particularly emphasized its evolution into "agentic AI." Agentic AI refers to AI that can understand user intent and autonomously execute multi-step tasks, suggesting that our daily digital assistants will become even smarter. It was also revealed that the highly anticipated next-generation smartphone, the Pixel 11, is scheduled for release this autumn. Pichai also made remarks hinting at collaboration with Apple and shared his own methods for utilizing AI. This paints a concrete picture of a future where AI permeates every domain, from individual lives to social infrastructure.

Impact on Our Lives: As agentic AI becomes widespread, we will be able to delegate more complex tasks to AI, leading to a dramatic improvement in daily work efficiency.

## China Mass-Producing 470 Short Dramas a Day with AI! Innovation in the Creative Field

As a noteworthy piece of news from "MIT Technology Review," which shares cutting-edge technology information from around the world, the mass production of short dramas using AI (Artificial Intelligence) in China has been highlighted. According to reports, an astonishing 470 short dramas are being produced daily in China through the skillful application of AI technology. This is a remarkable example of how rapidly generative AI is evolving and being commercially utilized in the field of video production. It significantly lowers the barrier to content creation, giving rise to a new form of entertainment industry that caters to diverse needs.

## Company-Wide "Generative AI Utilization Reskilling" Accelerates! Promoting DX with Practical Training

The promotion of DX (Digital Transformation) and the utilization of generative AI are now essential business challenges for companies. In this context, the news that Quark Co. has begun offering e-learning training for company-wide generative AI utilization reskilling (acquiring new skills) at a low price—approximately one-third of the industry standard—is noteworthy. This training, which allows employees to acquire AI skills directly applicable to their work at a cost of 100,000 yen per person, will significantly bolster generative AI adoption for many companies. Improving the overall AI literacy of employees directly contributes to strengthening a company's competitiveness.
