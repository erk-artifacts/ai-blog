## Arm Announces Its First Proprietary "AGI CPU" — Co-Developed with Meta for AI Specialization

Arm has announced "AGI CPU," its first proprietary in-house developed chip. This chip addresses growing demand for agentic AI (autonomous AI) and features high power efficiency and parallel processing capabilities.

Meta is playing a core role in the development, and the design is expected to be made public through the OCP (Open Compute Project) in the future. Over 50 companies including OpenAI and SocionNext have expressed support, and it is expected to become the standard for next-generation AI infrastructure.

**Impact on Our Lives:** More efficient and powerful AI processing will become possible, making everyday AI applications such as voice recognition, image generation, and autonomous driving faster and more energy-efficient.

## Kleiner Perkins Raises $3.5 Billion AI Investment Fund — Supporting Everyone from Startups to Growth Companies

Renowned venture capital firm Kleiner Perkins has announced the formation of a new $3.5 billion fund. The fund has made clear its commitment to concentrated investment in the AI sector, targeting a wide range of investment recipients from startups to growth-stage companies.

In terms of capital allocation, $1 billion will be invested in early-stage startups and $2.5 billion in late-stage growth companies. The rapid influx of capital into the AI industry will accelerate the birth of new companies and services.

**Impact on Our Lives:** With increased investment capital, AI-related startups will grow rapidly, and innovative AI products and services will appear in the market one after another.

## OpenAI's Sora App Shuts Down — AI Video Generation Model Continues

OpenAI has announced the discontinuation of "Sora," a social feed app exclusively focused on AI. While the underlying Sora 2 video and audio generation model itself will continue, the company determined there is no sustained demand for an app-based deployment.

While Sora 2's video and audio generation technology is sophisticated and has been called "eerily impressive," it became clear that a simple AI-only social feed has limited market potential.

**Impact on Our Lives:** It demonstrates that even cutting-edge technology cannot sustain itself without matching user needs, and AI companies are increasingly prioritizing practicality and market viability.

## Startup Strategy in the Generative AI Era — Serial Entrepreneur Kensuü Discusses "How to Build a Business That Won't Be Copied"

Kensuü (Kensuke Furukawa), a renowned serial entrepreneur and business influencer, has discussed IT startups in the generative AI era. His point is that with the rise of AI, the conventional wisdom of IT entrepreneurship is being fundamentally overturned, and the barriers to startup are rising.

## AI-Powered Compact Hearing Aid "Oticon Zeal" Released — No Ear Mold Needed, Take-Home Same Day

Oticon hearing aids by Demant Japan has released "Oticon Zeal," a compact hearing aid equipped with AI voice processing functions. Using pacemaker-derived capsulation technology, advanced features such as AI processing, charging capability, and Bluetooth connectivity are packed into an extremely compact form factor.

No ear mold is required, and same-day take-home is possible—a major convenience feature. AI-based voice processing achieves optimal sound adjustment according to the user's listening environment.

**Impact on Our Lives:** AI-enabled hearing aids allow people with hearing loss to experience more natural and comfortable sound, improving the quality of their social participation.

## Tourism Strategy-Specialized AI Platform "Tourism Intelligence" Launch Begins

Open Tone Inc. has begun offering "Tourism Intelligence," an AI platform specialized for tourism strategy. By collecting and accumulating data from tourism white papers and tourism promotion plans from all 47 prefectures nationwide, AI analyzes the data to support policy planning and decision-making for municipalities and DMOs (Destination Management Organizations).

By analyzing vast amounts of tourism-related data through machine learning (ML), necessary tourism promotion measures for regions can be derived through objective, data-driven methods.

**Impact on Our Lives:** AI analysis will optimize regional tourism, enhance visit experiences, and promote regional economic revitalization.
