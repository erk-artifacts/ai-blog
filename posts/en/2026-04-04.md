## Successive Management Changes at OpenAI, AGI Chief Takes Medical Leave

OpenAI is experiencing major personnel restructuring. Fidji Simo, who leads AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) deployment—not CEO Sam Altman—is taking medical leave. Simultaneously, COO (Chief Operating Officer) Brad Lightcap is assuming new duties as leader of a "special projects" initiative, and CMO (Chief Marketing Officer) Kate Rouch is expected to retire for cancer treatment.

These recent executive absences and reassignments highlight human resource management challenges as OpenAI experiences rapid growth.

## Anthropic Effectively Restricts OpenClaw Usage Through Paid Model

Starting April 4, Anthropic is implementing a new policy that prevents OpenClaw (third-party AI agents) from operating within Claude's usage allowance. Users will need to pay separately, making usage substantially more difficult.

Behind this move lies security vulnerability concerns with OpenClaw. Using OpenClaw could allow attackers to illegally obtain Admin privileges without requiring authentication, raising alarm among security researchers.

## Research Demonstrates Dangerous "Cognitive Surrender" Among AI Users Abandoning Logical Thinking

According to research reported by Ars Technica, LLM (Large Language Model) users tend to uncritically accept incorrect answers provided by AI without subjecting them to critical verification. This phenomenon, called "cognitive surrender," suggests that when many users become overly dependent on AI output, their own logical reasoning abilities may decline.

Experiments confirmed that the majority of users failed to question or challenge incorrect AI answers and simply believed them.

[→ Read original article (Ars Technica)](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/04/research-finds-ai-users-scarily-willing-to-surrender-their-cognition-to-llms/)

**Impact on our lives**: Over-reliance on AI risks weakening independent thinking abilities, leading to poor decisions based on incorrect information.

## Summary

As of April 4, the AI industry faces multiple critical junctures. Personnel changes are occurring simultaneously at OpenAI and Anthropic, the industry's two leading companies, while issues including Anthropic's biotech startup acquisition and OpenClaw security vulnerabilities have surfaced.

Conversely, research revealing "cognitive surrender" among AI users warns of declining human thinking abilities accompanying rapid AI adoption, making safe AI operation increasingly essential.
