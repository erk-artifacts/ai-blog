## The Future of AI Agents Revolutionizing Industries

The latter half of the insight report from Hannover Messe, one of the world's largest industrial trade fairs, has been released, revealing how AI agents (AI programs that autonomously perform specific tasks) and data utilization will shape the future of industry. AI agents are expected to accelerate efficiency and innovation in manufacturing and food and agritech sectors.

Impact on Our Lives: The way we work will become more efficient, and tasks that were previously cumbersome may be automated by AI.

## Alexa Evolves! New Feature Detects Amazon Impersonation Scams

Amazon US has added a new feature to its AI assistant, Alexa, that allows users to verify whether suspicious emails, calls, or messages claiming to be from the company are authentic. This strengthens the system for protecting users from risks such as phishing scams. This is a good example of how AI (Artificial Intelligence) supports our lives in terms of security.

Impact on Our Lives: We gain a powerful ally to protect ourselves from fraud in daily life, enabling us to use digital services with greater peace of mind.

## AI Infrastructure Company Crusoe Reportedly Raises Huge Funding at $30 Billion Valuation

Crusoe, an AI data center development company, has reportedly raised $3 billion (approximately 440 billion JPY) in funding at a valuation of $30 billion (approximately 4.4 trillion JPY). Infrastructure such as data centers, which support immense computing power, is essential for the evolution of AI (Artificial Intelligence), and this massive investment can be seen as a move to boost the growth of the entire AI industry.

Impact on Our Lives: The computing resources necessary for AI development will be further supplied, accelerating new AI services and research, and increasing the likelihood that more advanced AI technologies will reach us.
