## Summary

This week's AI forefront reveals how deeply AI technology is permeating research, business, and our daily lives. The establishment of an AI drug discovery startup by an OpenAI researcher and the use of AI agents in dating apps demonstrate that AI is beginning to play a role in increasingly specialized and intimate areas. Furthermore, high-performance gaming PCs now supporting AI processing suggest that individual-level AI utilization will continue to advance.
