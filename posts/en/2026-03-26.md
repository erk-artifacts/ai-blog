The leadership consists primarily of tech industry executives close to President Trump, including Meta CEO Zuckerberg and NVIDIA CEO Huang, clearly demonstrating an approach to advancing AI (artificial intelligence) and nuclear energy policy under business leadership. This framework has the potential to significantly impact how AI development and regulation unfold in the United States.

## Japan-Based AI Video Translation Service Adopted by Major Companies

The AI video translation service provided by Konnichiwa Hello, established in March 2024, is being successively adopted by Apa Hotels, major apparel companies, and television stations. The service has achieved a pricing model of "¥10,000 per 30 seconds," with Yasuhiro Hayami, Managing Director and COO, being a serial entrepreneur for the seventh time.

By addressing the new demand for video multilingualization, this service supports the international expansion of major companies. It represents a concrete example of AI technology contributing to solving real business challenges.

## Generative AI Evaluation Tool Visualizes Brand Assessment Across Multiple LLMs

Platter Inc. released the new "contextual analysis" feature on March 24 for "SUPER ACT," an LLMO (Large Language Model Optimization) analysis tool that visualizes brand evaluation in generative AI. By analyzing response data from multiple generative AIs including ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, it visualizes as scores the context in which companies and services are evaluated.

An LLM (Large Language Model) refers to a language processing model trained from vast text data. This tool enables quantitative understanding of how generative AI evaluates companies.

[→ Read original article (ASCII.jp)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/384/4384205/?rss)

**Impact on our lives:** By visualizing AI evaluation, companies can strategically improve, leading to more consumer-oriented business improvements.

## AI × Mathematical Optimization Automates Manufacturing Workplace Planning

MarkDoor Inc. began providing a "production planning optimization solution" on March 19 that automates planning in manufacturing. By leveraging AI and mathematical optimization techniques (computational methods that find optimal solutions under multiple constraints), it enables automatic generation of production and parts plans under diverse constraint conditions.

It achieves reduction of re-planning time and standardization of planning work, supporting the construction of production systems capable of responding to sudden scheduling changes. This is expected to significantly improve productivity and flexibility in manufacturing.

[→ Read original article (ASCII.jp)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/384/4384204/?rss)

**Impact on our lives:** Through manufacturing efficiency improvements, product delivery times and costs will be reduced, making it easier for consumers to purchase products.

## AI Skills Gap Widens: Income Disparity Between Experienced and Inexperienced Users Rapidly Expands

According to an Anthropic survey, while AI has not yet replaced jobs, a skills gap is rapidly expanding between early adopters and those without experience, which may amplify labor market inequality.

There is growing productivity and income disparity between "power users" who can master AI tools (such as ChatGPT) and those just beginning to use them. This may have serious implications for future employment markets, making education for everyone to adapt to the AI era an urgent necessity.

## Gemini Enterprise Enables MCP-Compatible Agent Development

Google Cloud's "Gemini Enterprise" now enables the development of agents (tools for AI to automatically execute tasks) compatible with MCP (Model Context Protocol). Agent development that works with CData Connect AI has been realized, making enterprise data integration more flexible.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) refers to a standardized protocol for safely linking AI with various systems and databases.
