## OpenAI and Other Employees Propose to U.S. Government: The Need to Adjust AI Development Pace

Over 1,000 employees from leading AI companies, including OpenAI and Google, have published an open letter asking the U.S. government for international support to adjust the pace of AI development. They point out the potential for uncontrollable risks as AI autonomy rapidly advances and emphasize the importance of developing tools necessary to control the development speed. This contrasts with efforts by companies to avoid regulation of open models, reflecting an active debate on AI safety and ethics.

## Anthropic's Top-Tier AI "Mythos" Discovers Vulnerability in Cryptographic Algorithm!

Anthropic announced it utilized its top-tier model, "Claude Mythos Preview" (Mythos), to discover mathematical flaws in cryptographic algorithms themselves. Mythos presented new methods that surpass conventional attacks against reduced versions of the quantum-resistant signature schemes "HAWK" and "AES." While there is no direct impact on production systems, this is a groundbreaking achievement, demonstrating AI's new potential in complex cryptanalysis and structural analysis, discovering possibilities that might be hard for humans to find.

Impact on Our Lives: AI could demonstrate its capabilities in the security field as well, bringing new perspectives to future cybersecurity measures.
