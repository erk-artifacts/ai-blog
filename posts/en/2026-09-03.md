## Nagano's Ways Holdings Introduces "AI President" to Combat Over-reliance on Individuals in General Affairs

Ways Holdings, based in Nagano Prefecture, which handles joint deliveries to major convenience stores, has introduced an "AI President" aimed at eliminating over-reliance on individuals in general affairs tasks (a state where only specific individuals can perform certain jobs). This AI is designed to reduce the workload and improve efficiency for management through data analysis and decision-making support. Particularly in departments with many routine tasks, such as general affairs, AI-driven automation is expected to be highly effective.

## Roboware Strengthens Total Solution Framework by Adding Three New Models

Roboware, a provider of automation solutions, has strengthened its total solution framework by adding three new robot models. This move will lead to smoother product supply and on-site implementation, contributing to the promotion of automation across various industries. ## be-FULL Co., Ltd., Provider of Management-Focused AI Agent "PX Cloud," Acquires SOC 2 Type I

be-FULL Co., Ltd., provider of the management-focused AI agent "PX Cloud," has acquired SOC 2 Type I (Service Organization Control 2 Type I), an international assurance report related to information security. This indicates that the company's services meet rigorous standards for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy, allowing users to utilize the service with greater peace of mind. AI agents like "PX Cloud" support management-level decision-making and contribute to improved organizational performance.
