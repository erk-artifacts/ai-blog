## OpenAI and Microsoft Knew of "Web Doom Loop" While Collecting Data

Data scraping has also been described as "the largest theft of labor in history."

**Impact on Our Daily Lives:** Discussions about copyright and ethics will further accelerate, focusing on how information we access daily on the internet is used by AI, and how content creators are affected as a result.
