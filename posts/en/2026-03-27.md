## Anthropic Wins Against Government Regulation, Obtains Preliminary Injunction Against Pentagon Restrictions

Anthropic has achieved a significant victory in litigation over regulations imposed by the Trump administration. A federal judge granted Anthropic's request and issued a ruling to temporarily block restrictions previously imposed by the Pentagon.

Following weeks of confrontation with the Pentagon, this ruling enables the company to continue operations while the judicial process to overturn the government's blacklisting proceeds. As an important precedent regarding AI companies and government regulation, it has potential to impact the entire industry.

**Impact on Our Lives:** By questioning the balance between regulation of AI companies and market competition in court, the development of AI technology and safety standards will become clearer in the future.

## Google Announces LLM Compression Technology "TurboQuant," Reducing Memory Consumption to One-Sixth

Google has announced "TurboQuant," a new technology that dramatically reduces memory consumption for large language models (LLMs). By combining "PolarQuant" and "QJL," this technology can compress KV cache (critical information referenced by the model) to 3 bits while maintaining accuracy.

Experimental results on NVIDIA's H100 (high-performance GPU) confirmed that computation speed is accelerated up to 8 times faster. Performance improvements are expected for Google's large-scale models like Gemini and vector search (technology for efficiently searching databases), potentially leading to significant reductions in AI inference costs (the process of generating predictions from trained models).

**Impact on Our Lives:** By making it possible to implement AI features at lower costs, advanced AI functionality on smartphones and edge devices will accelerate, and AI services will become more accessible while preserving privacy.

## Google Begins Offering "Chat Migration Tool" to Gemini, Supporting Transition from Other Generative AI

Google has begun providing a "migration tool" that allows users of other generative AI (such as ChatGPT) to easily switch to Gemini. This tool enables users to transfer their chat history and personal information directly to Gemini.

As the use of generative AI expands, infrastructure allowing users to freely move between multiple platforms contributes to promoting competition and improving user convenience. Simultaneously, it functions as Google's strategy to promote migration of users to Gemini.

**Impact on Our Lives:** AI users will find it easier to switch to tools best suited for them, and more active competition in the generative AI market is expected to lead to improved service quality.

## LINE Yahoo Chairman Kawabe Takes on Startups with AI, Declares "Completely Forgetting Internet Experience"

## Panasonic HD Begins External Provision of Vision Inspection AI Platform, Accelerating in Construction and Manufacturing

Panasonic Holdings has begun external licensing of a "vision inspection AI platform" developed by Panasonic R&D Center Singapore (PRDCSG), a subsidiary. This platform supports the development of AI for defect detection and quality inspection through image recognition (computer vision) across a wide range of fields including construction, infrastructure, and manufacturing.

In the vision inspection domain where each company previously developed AI models individually, providing a unified foundation achieves shorter implementation periods and cost reduction. This is an important step forward in digitalization and democratization of AI utilization in manufacturing.

**Impact on Our Lives:** As AI accelerates manufacturing quality improvement and production efficiency, it will become possible to purchase higher quality and safer products at more affordable prices.

## CureApp Adds Cerebrovascular Disease Risk Prediction Function to LINE "Dapan"

Medical startup CureApp has added a cerebrovascular disease (stroke, myocardial infarction, etc.) risk prediction function to the hypertension helper LINE account "Dapan." By inputting data such as blood pressure, age, and lifestyle habits, users can see the percentage risk of suffering from cerebrovascular disease within 10 years.

The fusion of preventive medicine and AI utilization is expected to improve individual health risk awareness and motivate lifestyle improvements and medical treatment. Implementation on LINE, a familiar platform, will accelerate the widespread adoption of AI-based medical services among general users.

**Impact on Our Lives:** AI predictions enable people to objectively understand their disease risk, allowing more effective engagement in preventive medicine, potentially leading to longer healthy lifespans.
