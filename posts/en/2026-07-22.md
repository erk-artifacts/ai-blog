## Block, Led by Jack Dorsey, Unveils AI Collaboration Platform "Buzz"

Block, led by Twitter co-founder Jack Dorsey, has unveiled "Buzz," an open-source platform where humans and AI agents (AI programs that perform specific tasks) can collaborate in the same workspace. Built on the decentralized protocol "Nostr," it features the ability to *freely integrate any LLM (Large Language Model) or AI agent*. By having each participant possess independent cryptographic keys, it aims to provide a secure collaborative environment.

Impact on Our Lives: New ways of working where AI and humans collaborate more seamlessly could spread, fostering innovation across various fields.
