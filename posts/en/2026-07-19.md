## Medical Prior Authorization Process: Will AI Improve or Worsen It?

The U.S. government is piloting a program that utilizes AI for prior authorization decisions before insurance coverage. While this endeavor holds the promise of streamlining medical administration and reducing costs, risks of AI unduly influencing patients' treatment choices are also noted. The introduction of AI particularly emphasizes transparency and fairness in this field.
