## Accelerating On-site Problem Solving with AI! Introducing "envedded"

The "envedded" on-site improvement platform, which utilizes AI, has launched its service. This platform supports efficient improvements by leveraging the power of AI (Artificial Intelligence) to address challenges such as labor shortages and productivity enhancement faced by companies with on-site operations, including manufacturing and construction. Specifically, AI analyzes and predicts various data collected from sites, visualizing where problems lie and how they can be improved, thereby helping to translate insights into concrete actions.

## Keeper Security Enhances Security, Ensuring Safety for Agent AI

Keeper Security has added control features for agent AI (AI that autonomously performs tasks) to its Endpoint Privilege Manager. In an age where AI automates tasks by collaborating with various systems, it is critically important to strictly manage AI's privileges to prevent unauthorized access or actions. With this new feature, companies can advance their use of AI while minimizing security risks.

Impact on Our Lives: Promoting the safe use of AI will lower the barrier to AI adoption for companies. As more services and products incorporate AI, our lives will become more convenient, while trust in security will also increase.

## Next-Generation AI Talent Emerges from Fukushima! "Fukushima AI Club" Kicks Off

It has been announced that AI company Xenkai will participate as an instructor in the "Fukushima AI Club," a high school AI talent development project promoted by Fukushima City and the Fukushima Digital Promotion Council. This initiative aims for high school students, who will lead the future, to learn AI (Artificial Intelligence) from basics to advanced applications and grow into individuals who can contribute to solving regional issues. Through practical education, it is expected that the younger generation will acquire AI skills and develop the ability to lead the digital society.
