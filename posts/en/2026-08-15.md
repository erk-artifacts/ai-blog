## AI Misuse in Court? Wrong Prompts Worsen the Situation
In a certain U.S. court case, a man representing himself, suspecting the court was using AI, intentionally embedded AI prompts (instructions for AI) in his submitted documents. This bizarre incident illustrates how the inappropriate use of AI chatbots (conversational AI) can exacerbate situations and lead to legal problems. The judge warned against this action as "misuse of a chatbot," once again highlighting the importance of understanding and appropriately using AI tools.
[→ Read the original article (Ars Technica AI)](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/suspecting-court-of-using-ai-man-injected-prompts-in-filings-to-try-to-win-case/)
Our impact on daily life: As AI becomes more prevalent, problems arising from its misuse or improper use are becoming apparent as social issues, underscoring the urgent need to establish legal frameworks and ethical guidelines.

## Google Gemini to Hide Watermarks on Generative AI Images
Google has started offering a new feature that allows users to hide watermarks displayed on images, videos, and music created with its generative AI tool, "Gemini." This can be done by toggling the "Media watermark" setting. However, even if this setting is turned off, *invisible benchmarks* to identify AI-generated files will still be applied. This aims to balance flexible use of AI-generated content with ensuring transparency regarding its origin.
[→ Read the original article (The Verge AI)](https://www.theverge.com/tech/980416/google-gemini-ai-watermarks-removal)
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/google-will-now-allow-users-to-remove-visible-watermark-from-its-ai-generations/)
Our impact on daily life: While generative AI creations become more freely usable, literacy in discerning the authenticity of AI-generated information will become even more crucial.

## Meta Releases Open-Weight AI Model "Glimmer" and the "AI for Everyone" Argument
Meta has released "Glimmer," an *open-weight AI model* that anyone can download and run on their own hardware. This contrasts with the company's more powerful model, "Muse Spark," which is locked behind an API (Application Programming Interface). Coinciding with this release, CEO Mark Zuckerberg published a letter asserting that "AI should be for everyone," emphasizing the importance of open AI development. This clearly articulates Meta's stance on democratizing AI technology.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/video/does-mark-zuckerberg-really-believe-ai-is-for-everyone/)
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/metas-open-ai-and-a-250m-deal-gone-very-wrong/)
Our impact on daily life: With an environment where anyone can access AI models and freely research and develop them, AI technology innovation could accelerate, potentially leading to the emergence of diverse AI applications.

## Kog's Technology to Maximize GPU AI Inference Performance
French startup Kog claims that the common perception of GPUs (Graphics Processing Units) not being suitable for *agentic workflows* (processes where AI agents autonomously perform tasks) is a misconception. They are developing technology to extract even more AI inference (using a trained AI model to make predictions or judgments) performance from GPUs. This indicates that optimizing hardware and software to improve AI processing capabilities remains a crucial theme.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/kog-is-going-deeper-to-squeeze-more-inference-out-of-GPUs/)
Our impact on daily life: Improved AI processing speed and efficiency are expected to lead to the widespread adoption of more advanced AI assistants and AI applications requiring real-time responses, enhancing the quality of our daily lives and work.

## OpenAI and Anthropic Enter Price War as Chinese Rivals Emerge
Major U.S. AI companies OpenAI and Anthropic are reportedly entering a price war, releasing cheaper AI models amidst the rise of Chinese AI competitors. This signifies the emergence of new challengers to their ambitious, *trillion-dollar scale* goals. As competition intensifies in the AI market, not only technological development but also pricing strategy is becoming a critical factor.
[→ Read the original article (Ars Technica AI)](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/openai-and-anthropic-in-price-war-as-chinese-ai-rivals-gain-ground/)
Our impact on daily life: Lowering AI service prices could make AI technology more accessible to a wider range of companies and individuals, further accelerating the societal implementation of AI.
