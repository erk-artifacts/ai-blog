## Anthropic CEO Amodei Advocates for "Pacing Adjustment" in AI Development

Dario Amodei, CEO of leading AI company Anthropic, has published an essay advocating for a slower pace of AI development due to AI's rapid self-improvement capabilities and associated safety concerns. He proposes a three-stage plan to regulate model capability improvements through the introduction of embedded third-party evaluators (experts who assess the safety and ethics of AI models) and international cooperation among democratic nations. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Elon Musk have also expressed support for this proposal, indicating an active debate among key players in the AI industry regarding AI development safety and control.

## Definition and Structural Hypotheses of "Invisible AI Misuse" Published, Re-evaluating Appropriate AI Use

As AI use advances, a new concept termed "invisible AI misuse" has been proposed. This refers to the possibility that, even when AI appears to be "in use," its application might hinder the growth of inherent human capabilities (such as critical thinking, creativity, and judgment) or lead to the atrophy of specific skills. The article presents a working definition and four structural hypotheses for this "invisible AI misuse," prompting deep reflection on how AI should be leveraged not merely as an efficiency tool but as a partner that fosters human growth.

[→ Read the original article (ASCII.jp Technology)](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/434/4434385/?rss)
When introducing AI into education and business, there will be a need to consider not just efficiency but also the impact on human capability development, leading to demand for more balanced AI usage guidelines.
