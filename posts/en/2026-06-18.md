## Generative AI-powered AI-OCR "GenOCR" Introduces Master Data Completion Feature Based on Similarity
GenOCR, a generative AI-powered AI-OCR (Optical Character Recognition) service, has released a master data completion feature based on similarity.
- Technical Terminology: AI-OCR (AI Optical Character Recognition) is a technology that uses AI to read handwritten and printed characters and convert them into digital data. Generative AI-powered means that it not only recognizes characters but also understands context and has the ability to perform more advanced processing and completion. The master data completion feature is a function where AI matches read data with existing master data (core data) and infers and completes missing or incorrect information.
This new feature allows data read from unclear documents, for example, to be automatically completed into more accurate information based on its similarity to existing master data. This is expected to significantly reduce data entry effort and errors.
Impact on Our Lives: The evolution of AI-OCR accelerates the digitization and efficiency of document-related tasks in businesses, improving the speed and accuracy of our information processing, and enabling accurate data utilization with less effort.
