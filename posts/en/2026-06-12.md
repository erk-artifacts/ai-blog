## Theker Raises $85 Million for General-Purpose Factory Robot Development
Theker has raised $85 million to develop general-purpose factory robots that do not specialize in specific tasks. Unlike the fixed forms seen in robots from companies like Boston Dynamics, Theker's robots feature a reconfigurable design, expected to offer flexible adaptation in various production environments.

## Jeff Bezos-Backed Prometheus Raises $12 Billion to Build 'Artificial General Engineer'
Prometheus, a physical AI startup backed by Jeff Bezos, has raised a massive $12 billion to build an "Artificial General Engineer" (AGE) for the physical world. The company aims to automate heavy industry engineering and pharmaceutical design, with its valuation reaching $41 billion. An Artificial General Engineer (AGE) refers to AI that is not limited to specific tasks and can autonomously solve a wide range of engineering problems.
[→ Read the original article (TechCrunch AI)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/jeff-bezoss-prometheus-raises-12b-to-build-an-artificial-general-engineer-for-the-physical-world/)
Impact on Our Lives: Complex engineering tasks and pharmaceutical development could accelerate dramatically, potentially having a significant impact on our health, infrastructure, and overall societal progress.
