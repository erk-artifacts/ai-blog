## Microsoft Announces Business Reorganization Centered on AI Agents and "Solara" Concept

Microsoft announced that it is redesigning its business around AI (Artificial Intelligence) agents. At its annual event, the company also unveiled "Solara," an AI device concept, envisioning a future where AI agents autonomously perform various tasks on behalf of users. This demonstrates Microsoft's strong intent to transform into a comprehensive platform company for the AI era, beyond just offering software.

Impact on Our Lives: Our way of working and interacting with devices will fundamentally change, and more personal, autonomous AI assistants will become widespread.

## Japanese Government Acquires Access Rights to AI "Mythos" to Strengthen Cyber Defense

The Japanese government has acquired access rights to the AI (Artificial Intelligence) system "Mythos" with the aim of strengthening its cyber defense capabilities. This indicates a growing national recognition that AI technology is indispensable against the increasing threat of cyberattacks. By utilizing AI, the ability to detect and defend against even more advanced and complex cyberattacks is expected to improve.

Impact on Our Lives: Our nation's security may be strengthened by AI, contributing to the realization of a safer digital society.

## Google Begins Testing New Feature Allowing Websites to Refuse Display in AI Search Results

Google has begun testing a new toggle feature within "Search Console" that allows website owners to choose whether or not their pages appear in AI (Artificial Intelligence) search results. This gives publishers (information providers) more control over how AI uses their content. Importantly, even if rejected with this setting, the content will still appear in regular search results, allowing publishers to maintain search traffic while securing content negotiation power.

Impact on Our Lives: Our everyday search engine experience will change, and new rules governing the relationship between web publishers and AI may emerge, potentially promoting access to more reliable information.
[→ Read the original article (ITmedia AI+)](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/2606/04/news071.html)

## AI Ad Optimization Tool "Cascade" Integrates with Shopify and Shopline to Support Maximizing EC Sales

The AI (Artificial Intelligence)-powered ad optimization tool "Cascade" has begun integration with e-commerce platforms Shopify and Shopline. This allows EC (Electronic Commerce) and D2C (Direct to Consumer) companies to centrally manage previously disparate ad data and store data, aiming to maximize sales through advanced AI analysis and optimization. Personalized ad delivery tailored to individual customers will become even more efficient.

Impact on Our Lives: The online ads we see will become more personalized, improving our e-commerce shopping experience, while companies can achieve more efficient marketing.
